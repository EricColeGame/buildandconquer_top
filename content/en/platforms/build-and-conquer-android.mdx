## Early Base Management: Power, Water, and Housing on Mobile

Every conquest begins on an empty plot of land. Before you ever invest in military research, your initial responsibility is establishing a self-sufficient domestic loop. Without foundational utilities and a stable workforce, factories grind to a halt and tax income plummets.

Power and water represent the twin pillars of urban viability. Energy keeps commercial properties trading and refineries operating, while reliable water pipelines satisfy residential basic needs. In community wiki data, residential units require direct water access before citizens agree to settle and take up employment.

```
Empty Plot ➔ Power Grid & Water Supply ➔ Residential Zoning ➔ Workforce ➔ Commercial Revenue
```

Touch controls make zoning straightforward, but compact building designs pay off over time. Placing utilities neatly along dedicated service roads keeps your industrial pollution away from high-density suburbs while preserving empty terrain for mid-game expansion.

Build in modest waves: place water infrastructure, build a power plant, zone two to three homes, and immediately pair them with cash-generating shops.

## Touchscreen Optimization and Performance Tips for Android

Navigating detailed strategy games on mobile devices requires unique layout adaptations. Without a physical mouse and keyboard, building intricate road intersections or targeting missile coordinate systems can feel demanding during chaotic server clashes.

* **Leverage Two-Finger Camera Rotation:** Use pinch-to-zoom and two-finger twists to check building boundaries before confirming placements. Misaligned roads create inaccessible plots that prevent workers from reaching stores.
* **Isolate High-Value Targets:** Keep Patriot batteries and power plants spaced apart. Grouping critical structures together makes your base vulnerable to splash damage from enemy ballistic strikes.
* **Keep Roads Wide and Symmetrical:** Leaving clear corridors ensures military vehicles navigate city blocks toward subterranean tunnels without snagging on residential curbs.
* **Monitor Battery Temps:** Extended sessions with high particle counts warm mobile processors quickly. Dimming screen brightness slightly keeps clock speeds stable during 8-player battles.
* **Form Alliances Early:** Open the in-game chat window to establish non-aggression pacts with adjacent players on the server. Dividing mainland defense duties lets you concentrate economic resources into specialized industrial manufacturing.

Because the game remains in active V0.1 Beta, mechanics receive regular patches from the developer. Keep in mind that exact construction costs, damage values, and unlock tiers change between builds as balance passes roll out.
