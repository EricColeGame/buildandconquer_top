### Power and Water Networks

Every structure requires electricity to remain operational, and residential zones cannot attract workers without a reliable water supply. If your power dips into a deficit, commercial buildings cease generating revenue, causing sudden economic collapse.

| Utility Structure | Community Wiki Classification | Primary Operational Output | Placement Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Residential Zoning and Workforce Growth

Citizens provide the labor pool required to operate commercial centers, extraction facilities, and assembly lines. When expanding your municipal territory on **Build and Conquer PC**, group housing strategically so roads can easily connect them to commercial services.

* **Starter Dwellings:** Poor House, Standard House, Suburban House, Two Story House. These structures demand minimal utilities and provide the foundational labor pool for your initial gas stations and shops.
* **Mid-Density Housing:** Low Income Apartment, Middle Class Apartment, Communal Housing 1. These facilities house higher worker volumes per square foot, making them ideal once your water networks stabilize.
* **High-Density Urban Living:** City Apartment, Mid Rise City Apartment. Maximize population density to unlock advanced city upgrades and supply labor for heavy military complexes.

### The Production Prerequisite Rule

A frequent point of confusion among beginners involves military production chains. For example, community reports note that researching weapons like the M16 at a Sloparms Office does **not** automatically equip your Barracks. 

After completing research, you must allocate production capacity in a **Military Factory** to physically fabricate the rifles using processed Iron. Only after your inventory registers the required number of manufactured weapons will your Barracks deploy armed personnel.

### Performance Optimization for Large Bases

As your metropolis progresses toward Level 4 or Level 5 status, hundreds of active buildings and missile trajectories can impact visual smoothness. To keep your framerate stable on **Build and Conquer PC**, apply these performance tweaks:

1. **Adjust Graphics Quality:** If heavy smoke from refineries or missile launch particles causes frame drops, switch Roblox graphics settings from Automatic to Manual and lower the slider to level 4–6.
2. **Disable Unnecessary Background Apps:** Streaming applications, web browsers with dozens of open tabs, and video encoders can starve your CPU of resources needed for background simulation.
3. **Use Grid-Aligned Zoning:** Laying straight road grids rather than winding paths prevents vehicle routing glitches and minimizes visual clutter on your plot.
4. **Group Defensive Enclaves:** Position Patriot Batteries and Iron Dome stations adjacent to high-value targets like Large Sloparms Offices and Refineries to maximize intercept efficiency.
