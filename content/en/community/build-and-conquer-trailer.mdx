## Strategic Tips for Your First Match

Stepping onto an empty plot can feel overwhelming when neighboring territories are already bristling with radar dishes and surface-to-air missile installations. Applying systematic planning principles observed in community guides will protect your early investments.

- **Plan for Grid Scalability:** Never build dead-end roads around your starting command building. Leave four-lane corridors open so heavy supply trucks can navigate from industrial parks to border checkpoints without creating bottlenecks.
- **Isolate Hazardous Facilities:** Fuel refineries and ammo depots explode when damaged. Placing them adjacent to residential districts or critical power plants risks secondary explosions that can level an entire city block.
- **Redundant Utility Networks:** Lay parallel power and water lines. If an enemy airstrike cuts a single transmission pylon, an alternate circuit will keep your point-defense turrets online.
- **Scout Neighboring Borders Early:** Deploy low-cost reconnaissance drones as soon as possible. Knowing whether your neighbor is prioritizing economic farms or rushing armored assault units dictates your early research priorities.
- **Form Mutual Defense Pacts:** Solitary nations struggle to withstand coordinated multi-front invasions. Negotiate non-aggression treaties or mutual defense agreements with adjacent players to secure shared borders.

By treating infrastructure maintenance and tactical vigilance as twin priorities, you can build a resilient, prosperous metropolis capable of weathering any military onslaught.
