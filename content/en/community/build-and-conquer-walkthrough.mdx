## Getting Started: Empty Plot to Self-Sustaining Settlement

Every match begins on an unbuilt plot with limited initial funds and a clean slate. Your primary objective in the opening minutes is not military recruitment, but establishing the core life-support loop: energy, clean water, housing, and basic commerce. Without power and water, residential neighborhoods cannot sustain citizens, leaving your commercial centers without the labor force required to generate revenue.

When laying out your first settlement, leave ample room for wide transit arteries. Roads connect your production hubs and allow transport vehicles to ferry goods across your territory. Place your initial power generators and water installations along the periphery of your residential grid so you do not crowd out future high-density expansion zones.

```
[Power / Water Grid] ---> [Residential Zone (Workers)] ---> [Commercial Zone (Cash Flow)]
```

According to community wiki data, early utility infrastructure relies on clean, modular setups before transitioning to heavy industrial plants:

| Structure Category | Available Buildings (Community Data) | Primary Function | Operational Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy Sources** | Solar Panels, Wind Turbines, Coal Power Plants | Powers urban and industrial structures | Space / Coal fuel for heavy plants |
| **Water Sources** | Water Tank, Desalination Factory | Supplies clean water to residential districts | Direct connection / Power input |
| **Early Logistics** | Basic Roads, Transport Nodes | Enables worker access and hauling | Continuous path layout |

Once your power generation and water supply are online, immediately begin zoning your initial houses. In this section of the **Build and Conquer walkthrough**, patience is vital: overbuilding commercial shops before housing creates empty storefronts that drain maintenance without generating profit.

## City Management: Balancing Population, Labor, and Commercial Revenue

Your city serves as the sole engine for your economic treasury. Unlike typical sandbox builders, revenue here is tied strictly to active employment. Residential buildings attract citizens and convert them into available workers. These workers automatically fill commercial buildings, which in turn yield regular financial returns into your national treasury.

Player experience confirms that expanding residential zones too rapidly without sufficient utilities causes immediate happiness penalties, stalling your city level progression. Upgrade your residential footprint incrementally, graduating from modest standalone homes to high-capacity apartment blocks as demand surges.

## Industrial Supply Chains: Raw Extraction to Consumer Manufacturing

You cannot buy military hardware outright from a menu; every rifle, transport vehicle, rocket launcher, and drone must be manufactured locally from raw resources. A dependable **Build and Conquer walkthrough** focuses first on securing natural deposits across your plot before constructing manufacturing centers.

Resource gathering centers around three core commodities: **Coal**, **Iron**, and **Oil**. Mines extract Coal and Iron, while Oil Pumps extract crude reserves from the ground. These raw inputs feed directly into your industrial production lines:

1. **Raw Extraction:** Mines produce Coal for power generation and Iron for structural frames, while Oil Pumps extract crude oil.
2. **Refining Process:** Small and Medium Refineries process crude oil into **Fuel**, which is essential for powering vehicles and military logistics.
3. **Consumer Goods Manufacturing:** Small Factories combine industrial materials to produce Consumer Goods, satisfying domestic markets and fueling higher-tier trade.
4. **Logistics Fleet:** Ground trucks haul refined materials between remote extraction points, refineries, and your central city warehouses.

| Industrial Facility | Input Materials | Finished Output | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mine** | Geological Deposits | Raw Iron & Coal | Feeds heavy industry & power plants |
| **Oil Pump** | Underground Reserves | Crude Oil | Fuels refining operations |
| **Small / Medium Refinery** | Crude Oil | Refined Fuel | Powers vehicles & armor |
| **Small Factory** | Iron & Raw Materials | Consumer Goods & Components | Supports city economy & arms lines |

Failing to balance your fuel supply is a common rookie mistake. If your refineries lack crude oil or power, your logistics fleet grinds to a halt, starving your military factories of the raw Iron required to construct equipment.

## Technology Research, Armament, and Base Defense

Once your economy generates an uninterrupted cash surplus, redirect resources into military research. Technological development takes place within specialized facilities known as Sloparms Offices. Investing in research unlocks military factories, allowing you to arm defensive garrisons, construct mobile armor, and prepare long-range ordnance.

Many players encounter confusion when staffing their Barracks. As noted in player community discussions, researching a weapon (such as the M16) only unlocks the production blueprint; you must actively manufacture those weapons in your military factories before your Barracks can equip deployed troops.

```
[Sloparms Office (Research)] ---> [Military Factory (Production)] ---> [Barracks / Launchers (Deployment)]
```

Defending your infrastructure is essential. Build and Conquer servers support up to 8 players, meaning hostile neighbors can coordinate strikes against your critical power plants or refineries. Community wiki data highlights several defensive and offensive structures:

Advanced players relying on this **Build and Conquer walkthrough** prioritize a layered defense. Place Iron Dome systems near your dense commercial hubs and cluster Patriot PAC-3 batteries around your industrial power plants to survive sudden missile attacks.

## Mainland Conquest: Tunnels, War Strategy, and Alliances

True endgame power in Build and Conquer revolves around mainland operations. Once your defensive perimeter is secure and your factories are stockpiling munitions, prepare your deployment pathways. Troops and mechanized units march through dedicated tunnels leading toward contested mainland objectives.

Because each server caps out at 8 nations, diplomacy plays a decisive role. Forming mutual non-aggression pacts or trade alliances early in the match allows you to pool military focus against aggressive players. When deploying forces toward mainland checkpoints, adhere to this operational checklist:

| Strategic Phase | Key Tactical Objective | Failure Risk / Bottleneck |

| **2. Air Defense Screen** | Overlap Patriot and Iron Dome rings near tunnel exits | Enemy drones eliminate units as they exit transit |
| **3. Reconnaissance Probe** | Launch Shahed drones to scout enemy utility grids | Blind assaults walk directly into entrenched artillery |

As highlighted in our **Build and Conquer walkthrough**, striking an enemy's utilities is far more effective than engaging in prolonged frontline skirmishes. Severing an opponent’s water networks or power stations immediately halts their factory production, collapses their commercial tax revenue, and renders their automated defense turrets inoperable.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most effective economic start in Build and Conquer?
The safest opening move is to build a modest power installation (such as Wind Turbines or Solar Panels) and a Water Tank before placing basic houses. Once citizens arrive, build starter shops and gas stations to generate immediate cash flow. Avoid building military offices or factories until your commercial revenue comfortably exceeds your recurring maintenance costs.

### Why says my Barracks I don't have weapons after researching them?
Researching a weapon blueprint (like the M16) at your Sloparms Office only unlocks the ability to build that item. You must construct a Military Factory and supply it with Iron to actively manufacture the weapons. Once physical weapon crates are produced, your Barracks can equip soldiers for field deployment.

### How do I level up my city to Level 2 and Level 3?
While exact thresholds remain unconfirmed by The Slop Company during Beta V0.1, leveling up requires maintaining high population happiness, complete water and power grid coverage, and a balanced worker-to-commercial ratio. If your city is not leveling up despite high population, check your utilities for localized blackouts or water shortages.
