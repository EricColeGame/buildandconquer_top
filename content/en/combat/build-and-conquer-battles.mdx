## The Economic Engine Behind Modern Warfare

Before you fire a single projectile or march soldiers toward the front lines, remember the foundational rule established by the developers: a strong economy wins wars, but a strong military protects what you built. In this game, combat strength cannot exist in a vacuum. Every bullet, chassis, launcher, and defense turret draws from the raw goods pulled from your land plots.

According to community wiki data and active player reports, the foundation for winning Build and Conquer battles rests on your domestic industrial output. If your civilian sector runs dry, your defense grids collapse, rendering your base an easy target for hostile artillery.

### Crucial Resource Chains

Military manufacturing relies on three core natural deposits: iron, coal, and crude oil. Without steady extraction routes, complex military factories cannot produce defensive munitions or offensive payloads.

*   **Iron Extraction:** Mines pull raw iron ore from the earth, which is routed directly to industrial foundries and Sloparms military facilities to construct vehicle frames and missile casings.
*   **Oil and Fuel Refining:** Crude oil extracted through pumps must pass through small or medium refineries to create refined fuel. Tanks, logistics trucks, and aircraft cannot operate on empty tanks.
*   **Energy and Water Stability:** Military infrastructure requires high electric capacity. If wind turbines, solar panels, or coal plants fail, anti-air tracking systems shut down immediately.
*   **Workforce Allocation:** Factories cannot run without personnel. Residential zoning (from small houses up to mid-rise city apartments) must have access to fresh water tanks or desalination plants to sustain the workforce running your assembly lines.

## Defensive Systems: Surviving Barrages and Air Raids

Once your economic foundation is secure, defending your metropolis during Build and Conquer battles demands an active, layered missile shield. Hostile players have access to a lethal array of long-range ballistic weaponry and loitering munitions. If you build dense commercial districts without perimeter air defense, an opponent can level your tax base in seconds.

Community reports highlight that defense is divided into point-defense systems and regional interceptor batteries. Placing them strategically around power hubs and resource silos ensures that collateral damage from stray hits does not knock out your entire electrical grid.

### Layering Your Air Defense Grid

1.  **Early Warning and Interception:** Community wikis report that systems like the Patriot Battery and advanced Patriot PAC-3 platforms specialize in tracking high-altitude ballistic trajectories. Position these installations near your city center to protect core commercial towers and town halls.
2.  **Short-Range Drone Shields:** Cheap loitering munitions like Shahed drones can bypass high-tier radar when fired in saturated waves. Incorporating regional interceptors like the Iron Dome provides close-in security against rapid, low-altitude strikes.
3.  **Physical Dispersion:** Never group your power plants, water desalination works, and military car storage facilities into one tight block. A single heavy missile payload can trigger chain reactions that wipe out your utility capacity.
4.  **Redundant Power Lines:** Keep secondary renewable generation (solar or wind) isolated from main industrial coal zones. If an enemy missile destroys your primary coal plant, backup generators keep defense batteries active.

## Offensive Operations: Missiles, Drones, and Mainland Invasions

When offensive Build and Conquer battles erupt across the map, waiting defensively on your plot is rarely enough to secure a victory. Servers support up to eight rival players, meaning stagnant empires eventually get outpaced by aggressive expanding neighbors. To defeat fortified nations, you must coordinate combined-arms strikes.

Offensive warfare centers on three distinct doctrines: strategic missile bombardment, unmanned drone saturation, and infantry or vehicle breakthroughs across mainland objectives.

### Research and the Sloparms Tech Tree

Military technology does not come pre-unlocked. Based on community wiki data, research facilities—including Small, Medium, and Large Sloparms Offices—are required to research advanced equipment. Crucially, research is frequently gated behind your overall city level. 

A common stumbling block noted in player guides involves weapon manufacturing pipelines. For instance, researching infantry gear like the M16 rifle at a research office does not instantly equip your barracks; you must actively dedicate factory lines to manufacture that equipment before your barracks can muster armed platoons.

### Strategic Missile Deployment

*   **Short-Range Ballistic Missiles (SRBM):** SRBM Launchers act as tactical bombardment tools designed to wipe out exposed border defenses, forward radar, and fuel depots.
*   **Medium-Range Ballistic Missiles (MRBM):** MRBM Launchers reach deep behind enemy lines, capable of targeting high-value infrastructure like refineries, military car storage depots, and apartment clusters.
*   **Hypersonic Weapons:** Late-game players frequently discuss hypersonic ballistic missiles. Their ultra-high velocity allows them to test even the best-defended Patriot PAC-3 networks, making them devastating initial-strike tools against hostile super-cities.
*   **Loitering Drones:** Shahed drones offer an economical way to harass enemy perimeters. Launching them in clusters ahead of high-value ballistic missiles forces opponent air defenses to expend interceptors on low-value targets.

## The Tunnel Network: Invading the Mainland

A distinctive mechanic in the game is the subterranean transit network. Players do not just exchange long-range missile salvos across the water; ground units deploy through specialized tunnels to reach the central mainland.

Executing coordinated Build and Conquer battles across the mainland requires disciplined timing. Rushing troops into a tunnel without logistical backup leaves them vulnerable to waiting defenses at the exit.

### Mainland Strike Checklist

1.  **Reconnaissance First:** Before committing vehicles or platoons, verify the exit environment. Ensure your target's surface defenses have been softened with artillery or drone barrages.
2.  **Combine Arms:** Never deploy foot soldiers alone. Support your ground forces with mechanized vehicles drawn from Military Car Storage facilities to absorb defensive fire.
3.  **Secure the Exit Portal:** Establish a fortified beachhead on the mainland side immediately. If enemy forces seal the tunnel opening, your offensive line will be cut off from supplies and reinforcements.
4.  **Target High-Value Nodes:** Focus ground attacks on the opponent's power transmission lines and water networks rather than low-value exterior farms. Disabling their grid shuts down automated base defenses instantly.

## Strategic Alliances and Server Politics

Because servers host up to eight competing players, diplomacy is just as vital as firepower. Entering Build and Conquer battles isolated against a united coalition of neighboring states almost always leads to defeat.

### Forming Alliances

*   **Complementary Specialization:** In multi-player matches, coordinate industrial roles. One ally can prioritize heavy refining and fuel stockpiles while another accelerates high-tier Sloparms missile research.
*   **Shared Defense Networks:** Interlocking air defense boundaries allow two nearby bases to create an impenetrable wall of Patriot and Iron Dome batteries, dividing ammunition costs while doubling interception rates.
*   **Synchronized Striking:** The most effective counter to modern anti-air batteries is saturation. When two allied states launch simultaneous missile barrages from different angles, defending target-acquisition systems become overwhelmed.

## Critical Tactical Mistakes to Avoid

A frequent blunder seen during Build and Conquer battles is building barracks and launching forward operations before your city's industrial supply lines are fully mature. If your economy cannot instantly replace downed missiles or destroyed vehicles, your counter-offensive will lose momentum rapidly.

Keep these survival principles in mind:

*   **Verify Barracks Weapon Supplies:** As noted by experienced players in community forums, simply completing an M16 research project does not supply your infantry. Check your military factory output to confirm that physical firearms are manufactured and delivered to your barracks before attempting an assault.
*   **Never Skimp on Desalination and Wells:** Workers manning your weapon lines require water. If municipal water reservoirs run low, residential happiness drops, worker quotas fall, and manufacturing grinds to a halt mid-fight.
*   **Avoid Over-Concentrated Generator Fields:** A single missile strike on a packed power field can leave your entire defensive network offline, leaving your factories completely unprotected. Spread your power generation across separate utility districts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes military production to stop during combat?
Military factories require a constant stream of raw iron, fuel, electricity, and civilian workers. If an opponent hits your refineries or disables your power stations, assembly lines stall immediately. Always safeguard your utilities behind air defense systems.

### How do I defend my base effectively during Build and Conquer battles?
Layer your air defenses. Use short-range Iron Dome emplacements to clear cheap drone swarms and incoming artillery, while reserving Patriot Battery and Patriot PAC-3 systems for heavy ballistic warheads. Disperse critical structures to prevent chain-reaction damage from stray explosions.

### Why won't my soldiers muster at the barracks?
Unlocking weapon blueprints in a Sloparms Office is only step one. Your industrial factories must manufacture the specific equipment (such as M16 rifles or chassis components) and route them to military storage before your barracks can deploy armed units.
