## Establishing the City Core: Water, Power, and Workforce

Developing a reliable **Build and Conquer strategy** begins with civic fundamentals. Your city is your sole engine for capital generation. In *Build and Conquer*, you cannot buy military assets directly without tax income, and taxes cannot be collected without commercial establishments staffed by working citizens. 

Every residential unit requires two continuous inputs to function: electrical power and clean water. If either utility dips into a deficit, residential output stalls. Unserviced houses will fail to supply the labor pool required to staff retail establishments, leading to empty cash registers and halted progress.

```
[Power Plants / Water Sources] 
             │
             ▼
    [Residential Zones] ──(Generates Labor)──► [Commercial Businesses] ──► [Tax Revenue]
```

Early settlement layouts should prioritize compact grids. Keep basic utility buildings near your perimeter to maximize contiguous space for high-density housing and commercial lots later on. 

### Core Municipal Building Classifications

Community wiki data highlights the initial structures available for baseline city planning:

| Category | Primary Buildings | Primary Resource / Role | Strategic Consideration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy Sources** | Solar Panels, Wind Turbines, Coal Power Plants | Power Generation | Coal plants offer reliable high wattage but demand physical coal truck deliveries. |
| **Water Supply** | Water Tank, Desalination Factory | Clean Water | Essential for unlocking worker population within residential zones. |
| **Residential** | Poor House, Suburban House, Low Income Apartment, Mid Rise City Apartment | Citizens & Laborers | Density scales with city level; higher tiers house more workers per tile. |
| **Commercial** | Shop, Gas Stations, Supermarkets, Office Buildings, Banks | Tax Revenue / Cash | Generates revenue per tick; completely inoperable without available workers. |
| **Agriculture** | Wheat Farm, Farm Field, Cattle Farm | Food Supply | Sustains growing citizen populations and prevents civil stagnation. |

A common hurdle reported in player experience involves hitting a progression wall between Settlement Levels 1 and 2. Players often construct hundreds of residential units, reach over 500 citizens, yet remain unable to advance. In almost all community reports, this bottleneck stems from widespread unemployment. Placing raw housing without matching commercial slots—such as Shops, Gas Stations, and Strip Malls—leaves hundreds of citizens jobless, preventing the settlement level algorithm from triggering an upgrade.

## Resource Extraction and Industrial Logistics

Your military will never roll off the assembly line without raw materials. Once your financial foundation stabilizes, your **Build and Conquer strategy** must shift toward raw extraction: Coal, Iron, and Oil.

Mines harvest coal and iron ore, while Oil Pumps extract crude petroleum. These raw inputs do not directly power military units; they must be transported along road networks into secondary refining facilities. Small and Medium Refineries process crude oil into vital Fuel, while Small Factories convert iron and coal into Consumer Goods and construction steel.

### Industrial Refining and Output Channels

Based on community wiki data, the industrial chain flows according to specific material conversions:

| Raw Extraction Site | Extracted Resource | Processing Facility | Intermediate & Final Outputs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mine (Coal Deposit)** | Coal Ore | Coal Power Plant / Small Factory | Power grid electricity, processed steel components |
| **Mine (Iron Deposit)** | Iron Ore | Small Factory | Consumer Goods, structural military materials |
| **Oil Pump** | Crude Oil | Small Refinery, Medium Refinery | Vehicle fuel, chemical rocket propellants |
| **Refineries** | Processed Fuel | Military Car Storage, Airfields | Mechanized unit deployment, continuous missile logistics |

Logistical pathing is a frequent pitfall. Numerous player reports cite instances where refineries and factories sit idle despite functional mines nearby. In *Build and Conquer*, physical transport trucks navigate your street layout to deliver goods. If roads feature dead ends, sharp non-snapped turns, or lack curb access directly bordering factory loading bays, delivery vehicles will fail to spawn or deliver cargo. Always verify that your extraction zones have unobstructed, two-lane connections leading directly to industrial refining districts.

## Military Research and Weapon Stockpiling

Research in the current Beta build dictates which armaments your nation can manufacture. Without specialized research, your production facilities will remain restricted to basic equipment. 

Research is conducted via Sloparms Office facilities (ranging from Small to Large). However, simply unlocking a blueprint does not magically grant usable gear in combat. A crucial **Build and Conquer strategy** for late-game combat readiness is managing your weapons cache before declaring war.

### The Barracks and Equipment Flow

A major source of confusion among new commanders is deploying infantry squads. When players research the M16 service rifle, they often expect immediate squad deployment from the Barracks. 

```
[Sloparms Office] ──(Unlocks Blueprint)──► [Military Factory] ──(Assembles M16s)──► [Barracks Deployment]
```

As demonstrated in recent player experience, military factories must physically assemble weapons using raw metal goods before infantry can mobilize. A Barracks requires a stockpile—such as 8 manufactured rifles—before a full fireteam can be sent onto the field or loaded into armored personnel transports.

## Defensive Layouts and Anti-Missile Systems

Offense may capture territory, but layered anti-air defense preserves your investment. Once neighboring players research ballistic rocketry, unsecured cities can be leveled in minutes. Executing an airtight **Build and Conquer strategy** against aggressive neighbors requires placing integrated radar and air-defense batteries before laying down expensive high-tier offices.

The game features several tiers of missile warfare and counter-battery systems:

*   **Short-Range Ballistic Missiles (SRBM Launcher):** Fast-firing, devastating to clustered municipal grids, but vulnerable to short-range point defense.
*   **Medium-Range Ballistic Missiles (MRBM Launcher):** High-arc ordnance capable of targeting deep interior factories and remote power grids.
*   **Iron Dome:** High-rate-of-fire interception system optimal for screening incoming low-tier rockets and loitering munitions like Shahed drones.
*   **Patriot Battery & Patriot PAC-3:** Heavy surface-to-air missile installations engineered to knock down incoming medium-range ballistic warheads before terminal descent.

### Threat Response and Defensive Matrix

Use this deployment matrix to balance your defensive budget against hostile tactics:

| Incoming Threat Type | Primary Interceptor Choice | Secondary Countermeasure | Base Layout Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shahed Loitering Drones** | Iron Dome | Forward Point-Defense Outposts | Disperse energy generators across separate grid cells |
| **SRBM Barrages** | Iron Dome / Patriot Battery | Rapid-Response Repair Crews | Decouple factories from residential cores to prevent collateral damage |
| **MRBM Heavy Strikes** | Patriot PAC-3 | Redundant Air Defenses | Place Patriot batteries near central command assets |
| **Ground Troop Incursions** | Fortified Barracks Garrisons | Armored Patrol Vehicles | Build choke points and tunnel exits near exterior plots |

Position your missile defense installations on the perimeter of your primary industrial sector rather than directly adjacent to your town hall. This ensures incoming projectiles are intercepted while still over no-man's-land, avoiding falling debris or splash damage to your water reservoirs and power stations.

## Strategic Roadmap: Settlement to Regional Power

To prevent wasteful spending and avoid the progression traps common in the early access build, follow a phased developmental cycle. Refining your long-term **Build and Conquer strategy** means knowing when to prioritize internal infrastructure and when to expand military capabilities.

### Development Phases Overview

| Progression Phase | Target Milestone | Economic Goal | Military Posture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Settlement Genesis** | City Level 1–2 | Build Water Tanks, Solar Panels, Poor Houses, and initial Shops | Demilitarized; focus purely on cash-positive cash flow |
| **Phase 2: Industrial Extraction** | City Level 2–3 | Connect Coal/Iron Mines and Oil Pumps to clean two-lane roads | Construct initial Sloparms Office; begin basic weapon research |
| **Phase 3: Munitions Assembly** | City Level 3–4 | Construct Small Factories, Refineries, and mid-tier Apartments | Stockpile small arms in Barracks; deploy early patrol vehicles |
| **Phase 4: Fortified Sovereignty** | City Level 4–5 | Deploy High-Rise Commercial towers, Banks, and Desalination plants | Build Iron Dome and Patriot PAC-3 networks; construct SRBM silos |

Remember that land expansion requires significant financial reserves. Do not purchase peripheral plots until your current territory operates at maximum employment efficiency with balanced power, agricultural reserves, and water redundancies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best early-game Build and Conquer strategy for fast cash?
The fastest way to generate income is maintaining a 1:1 employment ratio. Construct basic residential housing connected to functional water sources, and immediately build Shops or Small Gas Stations along the main roadway to absorb that workforce. An employed population generates high tax yields, whereas unemployed citizens drain city logistics without returning capital.

### Why won't my settlement upgrade past Level 1 or Level 2 despite having high population?
According to player experience and community reports, leveling up requires balanced civic metrics rather than raw headcount. If you have 500 residents but most are unemployed, lack clean water, or lack road access to commercial zones, your city grade will stall. Ensure power generation exceeds consumption, water covers all residential lots, and sufficient commercial spaces exist to employ your citizens.

### Why are my mines and oil pumps not delivering resources to my factories?
This issue usually traces back to your road layout. Resource delivery trucks require uninterrupted road connectivity between the extraction site and the target factory. Ensure that building entrance arrows align directly with adjacent road tiles and that there are no gaps, broken intersections, or unpaved terrain between your oil pumps, mines, and refineries.

### How do I train and deploy infantry from the Barracks?
Researching a weapon blueprint (such as the M16) at a Sloparms Office is only step one. You must then build a Military Factory to fabricate those weapons using your steel reserves. Once the required number of weapons has been manufactured and delivered to your Barracks inventory, you will be able to spawn fully armed rifle squads for defense or transport operations.
