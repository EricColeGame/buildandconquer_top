## Understanding Missile Warfare in Build and Conquer

Long-range ordnance in the game serves as your primary tool for strategic disruption. While infantry and armored divisions seize ground on the mainland, missile artillery bypasses defensive frontlines to strike high-value facilities directly across the map.

Missile platforms fall into distinct operational tiers. Early conflicts rely on tactical short-range bombardment to soften perimeter outposts. As your economy scales and research hubs expand, heavier ballistic systems come online to threaten industrial sectors deep inside opposing territories.

Community reports identify several standard and specialized launch platforms currently integrated into the military tech tree:

| Launcher System | Platform Role | Target Profile | Key Operational Characteristic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SRBM Launcher** | Tactical Short-Range | Frontier defenses, outposts | Fast assembly cycle, low industrial resource footprint |
| **MRBM Launcher** | Strategic Medium-Range | Industrial zones, city centers | Heavy payload capable of leveling dense structures |
| **Hypersonic BM** | Ultra-High-Speed Heavy Strike | Hardened infrastructure, command bases | Extremely high velocity designed to overwhelm standard interceptors |
| **Iskander Battery** | Premium Tactical Ballistic | Mobile armor columns, factories | Specialized launcher unlocked through the Eastern European Pack |

Deploying **Build and Conquer missiles** requires more than just placing a launcher pad on an open grass tile. Every silo requires a stable civil power grid, dedicated munitions manufacturing, and sufficient municipal workforce levels before a single rocket can leave the tube.

## How to Unlock and Research Missile Tech

You cannot rush missile silos on a brand-new plot immediately. Military progression in *Build and Conquer* is closely tied to your municipal infrastructure and urban population milestones. 

To access the foundational military research tree, player reports confirm that your settlement must first achieve Level 2 status by reaching at least 250 verified residents. Achieving this requires building sufficient housing—such as mid-rise apartments or suburban homes—supported by uninterrupted water tanks and electric power stations.

```
City Growth (250 Residents) ➔ Research Offices (Sloparms) ➔ Munitions Factories ➔ Ballistic Launchers
```

Once your civic foundation is solid, you must construct dedicated research buildings known as Sloparms Offices. These facilities process research points to unlock advanced platforms:

| Progression Step | Infrastructure Required | Resource / Unlock Milestone | Output Unlocked |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1: Base Mobilization** | Mid-Rise Housing, Utilities | Reach 250 Citizens (Level 2 City) | Basic Barracks & Military Factory |
| **Step 2: Tactical Ballistics** | Small / Medium Sloparms Office | Refined Steel & Military Tech Points | SRBM Launcher Platform |
| **Step 3: Strategic Ballistics** | Large Sloparms Office | Advanced City Level & High Power Grid | MRBM Launcher Platform |
| **Step 4: Hypersonic Weaponry** | Advanced Research Complex | 100 Verified Direct Missile Hits | Hypersonic BM Launch System |

According to community wiki records, unlocking the devastating Hypersonic BM is unique because it cannot be bought with standard currency alone. Players must actively engage in multiplayer combat and register 100 direct missile hits against hostile ground units or player buildings using lower-tier SRBMs and MRBMs to finalize the research project.

## Building the Industrial Supply Chain for Missile Production

A missile launcher without ammunition is merely an expensive landmark. Keeping your silos armed during sustained server wars demands a robust domestic manufacturing chain that links raw earth extraction directly to automated assembly lines.

Missile components demand three primary raw materials: Coal, Iron, and Crude Oil. If any single component in your logistics web stalls, missile production halts instantly, leaving your city defenseless against retaliation.

### Refining Munitions Components

To maintain steady production of **Build and Conquer missiles**, lay out an organized industrial corridor connected by paved road networks. Raw oil must flow directly into Small and Medium Refineries to generate processed fuel, while raw iron is converted into structural casing steel.

| Facility Name | Category | Primary Input | Manufactured Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oil Pump** | Extraction | Ground Well Reserve | Crude Oil |
| **Mine** | Extraction | Subsurface Deposits | Iron Ore & Coal |
| **Medium Refinery** | Processing | Crude Oil | Vehicle & Rocket Fuel |
| **Large Military Factory** | Heavy Assembly | Steel, Processed Fuel, Parts | Rockets, Munitions, Chassis |
| **Commercial Supermarket** | Commercial | Workers, Road Access | High Cash Flow for Maintenance |

The Large Military Factory serves as the central hub for munitions fabrication. Ensure this facility has dedicated access to heavy electrical power from Coal Power Plants or Wind Turbines. If your grid browns out, manufacturing timers reset, creating critical ammunition shortages during live sieges.

## Strategic Missile Strikes: Bombardment and Mainland Tactics

Firing blindly into enemy territory wastes valuable munitions. High-tier players analyze enemy city layouts to cripple key infrastructure rather than targeting decorative civilian suburbs.

In the official [Build and Conquer Roblox experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/103767961452227/Build-and-Conquer), bases connect to a contested mainland zone through transit tunnels. When players push forces through these tunnels to secure revenue-boosting control points, their bases become active targets for server-wide player-versus-player retaliation.

### Target Prioritization Matrix

When coordinating salvos with alliance members, synchronize strikes to strike interdependent municipal nodes in rapid succession:

| Target Priority | Target Building | Strategic Impact on Target City | Recommended Ordnance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Priority 1** | Power Plants (Coal / Solar) | Disables air defense radars and assembly lines | Concentrated SRBM Volley |
| **Priority 2** | Water Towers / Desalination | Drops resident happiness and sparks rapid population decline | MRBM Salvo |
| **Priority 3** | Large Military Factories | Freezes enemy vehicle and missile replenishment | Hypersonic BM Strike |
| **Priority 4** | Supermarkets / Commercial | Cuts off tax revenue needed for upkeep | Standard Ballistic Rockets |

Coordinating your launch sequence is critical. Firing single missiles allows automated point-defense turrets to eliminate incoming rockets with minimal effort. To bypass heavy anti-air umbrellas, launch saturation barrages: fire cheap SRBM rockets or drone waves first to bait interceptor batteries, immediately followed by heavy MRBMs or Hypersonic BMs aimed at primary utility structures.

## Countering Missile Barrages: Point Defense and Interceptors

Surviving rival salvos requires a layered defense grid. Relying on a single anti-missile system leaves blind spots that experienced adversaries will quickly exploit using angled launch trajectories.

*Build and Conquer* features multiple active protection and surface-to-air missile platforms designed to track and swat down incoming projectiles before impact:

| Defense System | System Class | Interception Profile | Practical Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron Dome** | Short-to-Mid Point Defense | High-rate interceptor for rockets & drones | Vulnerable to large simultaneous swarms |
| **Patriot Battery** | Area Defense System | Mid-altitude ballistic missile tracking | Requires high electrical power allocation |
| **Patriot PAC-3** | Advanced Interceptor | Heavy ballistic & high-velocity projectiles | Higher research and placement cost |
| **Sea RAM** | Close-In Weapon System | Terminal-phase rocket point defense | Restricted placement limit (Max 6 per base) |
| **S-300 / S-400 / S-500** | Long-Range Air Defense | Ballistic & high-altitude strike craft | Available through Eastern European Pack |

Community testing indicates that placing point defenses haphazardly produces overlapping gaps. Position point defenses like the Iron Dome and Patriot PAC-3 directly adjacent to your energy grid and industrial hubs. 

Because player reports verify that Sea RAM installations are limited to a strict cap of six units per base, reserve these precious installations for your most critical assets: your Large Military Factory and your primary missile silos.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Build and Conquer Missiles

### How do I unlock the Hypersonic BM in Build and Conquer?
According to community wiki records and verified player testing, unlocking the Hypersonic BM requires scoring 100 confirmed hits on enemy ground units or buildings using earlier missile types like SRBMs and MRBMs. Once this combat requirement is fulfilled and your Sloparms research offices process the tech, the launcher becomes available for construction.

### What is the best way to defend my base against Build and Conquer missiles?
The most reliable defense strategy is constructing a layered interceptor network combining Patriot PAC-3 batteries with Iron Dome installations. Supplement these with the maximum allowance of six Sea RAM point-defense units concentrated tightly around your power plants and military factories to catch projectiles that slip through high-altitude interception.

### Why are my missile launchers not firing in Build and Conquer?
If your missile batteries refuse to fire, verify three common failure points: confirm your city has sufficient power output without rolling blackouts, ensure your Large Military Factory has steady deliveries of refined fuel and steel to assemble ammunition, and make sure your target is within the operational range of that specific launcher class.

### Does purchasing game passes unlock extra missile systems?
Yes, community reports confirm that purchasing the optional Eastern European Military Pack grants immediate access to specialized regional equipment, including Iskander missile batteries as well as advanced defensive missile networks such as the S-300, S-400, and S-500 systems. Standard missile platforms like the SRBM and MRBM remain earnable through normal gameplay research.
