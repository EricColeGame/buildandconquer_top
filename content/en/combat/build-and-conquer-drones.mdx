## Unlocking Build and Conquer Drones: City Milestones and Requirements

In the current V0.1 beta developed by The Slop Company, military technology cannot be rushed in isolation. Community wiki data confirms that all advanced weaponry is gated behind civic expansion. You cannot access aerial manufacturing until your settlement reaches City Level 2, which requires sustaining a thriving urban base.

According to player reports, reaching City Level 2 requires hitting a threshold of roughly 250 residents. While that number might sound modest, citizens demand active utilities, road connections, and commercial jobs. If your city suffers from blackouts or water shortages, population growth freezes, preventing the military research tree from opening.

Once you cross the population threshold, your City Hall unlocks the Military Projects interface. Crucially, the tech tree requires vertical progression: you must research entry-level ground units and missile systems before the drone node becomes available. Attempting to stockpile cash for aerial units without clearing prior military tiers will leave the drone project permanently locked.

| Prerequisite Metric | Community-Reported Threshold | Strategic Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **City Level** | Level 2 | Unlocks the military research interface in City Hall |
| **Sustained Population** | ~250 Residents | Triggers civic level upgrade; requires housing and jobs |
| **Treasury Reserve** | $5,000+ Cash Surplus | Covers the upfront cost to start drone research |
| **Electrical Stability** | 95%+ Grid Coverage | Prevents residential happiness drops and factory brownouts |
| **Water Infrastructure** | 90%+ Utility Coverage | Keeps apartment occupancy capped at maximum capacity |
| **Tech Tree Node** | Preceding Missile Research | Mandatory bridge project before aerial research unlocks |

Review your budget before clicking the research button. Player reports highlight that military research projects drain operating cash every in-game minute while active. If your commercial tax revenue is too low, the research project will drain your treasury into negative numbers, halting city production entirely.

## Optimal Early Build Order to Fast-Track Drone Research

Securing **Build and Conquer drones** before your neighbors requires a disciplined opening build order. Pouring money into heavy industry or sprawling road grids too early will exhaust your starter funds. Instead, focus on dense residential zones backed by quick-earning retail stores.

Start by placing a two-lane paved thoroughfare off your central spawn point. Drop two standard Supermarkets or basic Shops along this road to generate immediate sales tax. Once your initial residents move in, reinvest your first tax returns directly into basic utilities—a Coal Power Plant and a Water Tank. Community testing shows that Coal Power Plants provide the most dependable early-game megawatt output, avoiding the multi-stage resource chains required by late-game nuclear options.

Layer your residential blocks using Mid-Rise City Apartments rather than low-density single houses. Apartments provide the highest population density per square tile, letting you clear the 250-resident requirement on a compact utility footprint.

```
[Main Road] 
  ├── Commercial Hub (2x Supermarkets / Gas Station)
  ├── Residential Sector (3x Mid-Rise Apartments)
  └── Utility Grid (1x Coal Power Plant + 1x Water Tank)
```

| Phase | Recommended Construction | Economic Purpose | Milestones Achieved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Inception** | 2x Supermarkets, 2x Houses, 1x Coal Plant | Generates initial cash; establishes base power | Stable early income, ~30 population |
| **Stage 2: Urban Density** | 3x Mid-Rise Apartments, 1x Water Tank | Packs workforce into a tight utility corridor | Crosses 120 population; tax baseline |
| **Stage 3: Commercial Expansion**| 1x Strip Mall or Bank, 1x Large Supermarket | Multiplies retail profits to fund research fees | $400+ per tick cash flow |
| **Stage 4: Civic Threshold** | Additional Mid-Rise housing, Sewage utility | Pushes population past the City Level 2 gate | 250 residents reached; unlocks Military tree |
| **Stage 5: Tech Transition** | Basic Missile Project, Drone Research Project | Clears required tech prerequisites | Unlocks Drone Hangar and production |

Once your treasury crosses the $5,000 surplus mark and the population stabilizes above 250, pause secondary municipal expansion. Dedicate all surplus revenue to clearing the missile prerequisite, then immediately initiate the drone project. The research timer takes approximately 8 to 12 in-game minutes under standard server settings.

## Drone Types, Tactical Roles, and Aerial Warfare Mechanics

Once research completes, you can construct the Drone Hangar or specialized drone launch facilities on flat terrain connected to your road network. In the V0.1 beta, **Build and Conquer drones** fulfill distinct combat roles ranging from persistent reconnaissance to heavy anti-armor saturation.

The community wiki notes specific production units like the Shahed Drone alongside various combat airframes. Because air units fly above standard terrain obstacles, they bypass natural barriers like rivers and cliffs, striking directly at an adversary's vulnerable resource pumps and electrical substations.

| Drone Type / Role | Reported Strengths | Primary Battlefield Function | Optimal Target Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scout Drone** | High movement speed, cheap replacement cost | Clearing fog of war; revealing hidden missile pads | Enemy borders and unmapped sectors |
| **Shahed / Loitering Munition** | High kinetic impact, one-way strike trajectory | Breaching fortified perimeters; static target strikes | Power plants, refineries, command posts |
| **Combat Drone** | Reusable payload, balanced speed and durability | Harassing light vehicle columns and infantry squads | Troop convoys and isolated claim posts |
| **Heavy Strike Drone** | Reinforced durability, heavy payload capacity | Cracking heavy armor and industrial infrastructure | Military factories and heavy vehicle depots |
| **Stealth Platform** | Low radar visibility signature | Surgical deep strikes behind active air defenses | Early warning radars and SAM batteries |

Deploying **Build and Conquer drones** effectively requires understanding their operational limits. Unlike tanks, aerial drones have limited health pools and can be torn apart by concentrated anti-air fire. Never deploy strike drones in a trickle against a defended position. Instead, send a cheap scout drone ahead to trigger and identify anti-air coverage zones, then route your strike package through defensive blind spots.

Coordinate drone sorties with your ground army. If you have troops advancing through underground tunnels toward an enemy sector, launch an aerial strike seconds before they emerge. The drone bombardment forces the defender to scramble, pulling fire away from your emerging infantry and light vehicles.

## Base Defense: Countering Enemy Drone Swarms

Offense is only half the battle. If your opponent unlocks **Build and Conquer drones** first, an unfortified city can lose its power grid and industrial factories within minutes. Establishing an overlapping air defense umbrella is essential for protecting your civic investments.

City tiles feature independent defense stats separate from civic happiness. Leaving an industrial border tile at baseline defense invites aerial harassment. You must deploy static surface-to-air defense structures to interdict hostile aircraft before they reach your urban core.

| Defensive Structure | Strategic Combat Role | Best Placement Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron Dome** | Intercepts incoming light munitions and rapid air strikes | Centered directly over dense commercial and residential cores |
| **Patriot Battery** | Medium-to-long-range anti-air engagement umbrella | Border perimeters facing rival nation boundaries |
| **Patriot PAC-3** | Advanced heavy interdiction against high-tier munitions | Guarding high-value assets (City Hall, Military Factories) |
| **Patrol Drone Hangar** | Autonomous airborne interceptors hunting hostile scouts | Positioned behind frontline SAM sites to cover radar gaps |

According to player experience in multiplayer matches, the most resilient defensive layout follows a layered concentric ring. Place long-range Patriot batteries near your forward claim posts to engage targets early. Back them up with centrally located Iron Dome systems to catch fast loitering munitions that slip through the perimeter. 

Always keep backup power lines running into your anti-air grid. If an enemy strike cuts the single power line feeding your Patriot battery, your entire defense network collapses, leaving your hangars open to destruction.

## Common Drone Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

Even experienced city planners make mistakes when expanding into aerial combat. Avoiding these common community-reported pitfalls will protect your treasury and your air fleet:

*   **The Multi-Research Cash Trap:** Researching drones while simultaneously queuing missile and vehicle upgrades drains thousands of dollars per minute. Always research one military project at a time and maintain at least $10,000 in your reserve before starting.
*   **Unprotected Drone Hangars:** Placing your manufacturing facilities on the border makes them prime targets for early artillery raids. Keep your hangars deep in your territory behind air-defense umbrellas.
*   **Neglecting Fuel and Resources:** High-tier military production requires refined oil and iron. Failing to build Oil Pumps and Small Refineries before producing drones can stall production lines mid-match.
*   **Blind Strikes into Anti-Air:** Blindly right-clicking an enemy City Hall with strike drones usually results in total fleet destruction. Always probe enemy airspace with cheap reconnaissance to map out Patriot and Iron Dome coverage before committing expensive units.
*   **Ignoring Territorial Claims:** Drones cannot capture territory on their own. They are combat platforms, not expansion tools. Always escort ground engineers or place Claim Posts manually to secure newly cleared land.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to unlock Build and Conquer drones in the V0.1 beta?
Under normal server progression, reaching the 250-resident requirement takes approximately 20 to 35 in-game minutes of focused urban planning. Once the population gate is cleared, researching the mandatory missile prerequisite and the drone project adds another 8 to 12 minutes, depending on your economic stability.

### Can Build and Conquer drones capture enemy tiles automatically?
No. Drones are strictly tactical combat units designed for scouting, structural demolition, and fire support. They cannot claim neutral or hostile sectors. To expand your borders, you must manually place Claim Posts and connect them to your infrastructure using ground forces.

### What is the most effective counter to early-game drone rushes?
The most reliable counter is early investment in static air defense. Constructing an Iron Dome system over your central production hub alongside Patriot batteries on your border will neutralize incoming light airframes before they can hit critical municipal power plants.

### What happens to my drones if I temporarily lose power?
If your power plants are destroyed or disconnected, your Drone Hangar will cease production of replacement airframes. However, existing drones already deployed on patrol or loitering routes will remain active until they are shot down or run out of mission commands. Restoring the electrical grid is critical to resuming unit repairs and replacements.
