## The Foundation: Powering Your War Machine with Civil Infrastructure

In the modern theater of war, an army marches on its industrial budget. In Build and Conquer, your primary revenue engine is your civilian metropolis. Before placing your first defensive battery or barracks, you must establish self-sustaining energy, fresh water, residential zones, and commerce.

Citizens provide the civilian labor force required to operate commercial outlets and industrial refineries. Commercial buildings generate the consistent cash flow needed to purchase military blueprints, expand territory plots, and manufacture advanced armaments. If your population is dehydrated, starving, or without electricity, productivity halts, leaving your military installations underfunded and helpless.

### Balancing Urban Utilities and Commercial Zones

Every residential structure—from basic Poor Houses to dense Mid Rise City Apartments—demands steady water and power connections. Community reports emphasize that city level progression depends heavily on maintaining high employment rates and balancing housing with commercial capacity. Placing shops and gas stations without enough workers will stall your economic progress.

| Utility / Sector | Community-Reported Buildings | Core Purpose | Logistical Impact on Military |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy Sources** | Solar Panels, Wind Turbines, Coal Power Plants | Supplies baseline grid electricity | Prevents military factories and radar grids from shutting down |
| **Water Supply** | Water Tank, Desalination Factory | Hydrates residential sectors | Ensures residential capacity stays active to supply industrial labor |
| **Agriculture** | Wheat Farm, Farm Field, Cattle Farm | Generates food supplies for citizens | Keeps civilian stability and city growth metrics steady |
| **Commercial** | Shops, Gas Stations, Banks, Supermarkets, Offices | Generates continuous cash income | Provides operational funding for troop recruitment and research |
| **Housing** | Suburban Houses, Two Story Houses, City Apartments | Houses citizens and creates workers | Fills required staffing quotas for manufacturing facilities |

Always plan road layouts carefully when designing residential and shopping districts. Community feedback highlights that smooth road connectivity is mandatory for logistical flow and city upgrades.

## Logistics and Supply Lines: Fueling the Build and Conquer Army

A well-equipped **Build and Conquer army** requires three indispensable raw resources: Coal, Iron, and Crude Oil. Without a functional supply chain connecting natural extraction nodes to refineries, your weapons foundries will sit completely idle.

Players frequently experience logistics hurdles where raw resources fail to reach processing plants. In the current Beta release, all extraction facilities—such as Mines and Oil Pumps—must have unobstructed, unbroken road access directly connected to industrial plants and refineries. If heavy cargo trucks cannot chart a valid navigation path along paved roads, raw materials will accumulate at the wellhead while your factories starve for parts.

### Raw Resource Processing and Refining

Once raw commodities are harvested, secondary industrial facilities convert them into usable supplies. Crude Oil is converted into Fuel inside refineries, which is necessary to keep combat transport and mechanized armor running. Iron and Coal feed heavy foundries to forge structural steel and ballistic casings.

| Industrial Facility | Raw Input Required | Processed Output | War Effort Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mine** | Earth Extraction Nodes | Iron Ore, Coal | Foundational raw materials for ammunition, armor plate, and power |
| **Oil Pump** | Subterranean Petroleum Wells | Crude Oil | Essential feedstock for vehicle fuels and advanced chemical propellants |
| **Small / Medium Refinery** | Crude Oil | Refined Fuel | Powers motorized transport, drone sorties, and tactical strike vehicles |
| **Small Factory** | Iron Ore, Refined Goods | Consumer Goods & Parts | Supplies assembly components required by military design bureaus |

If your factories show zero production despite full stockpiles at your mines, player experience suggests inspecting every junction on your road network. Deleting and re-snapping the connecting road tile often resets the truck delivery AI pathing.

## Military Research and Sloparms Development

You cannot build modern heavy armor or ballistic launchers right out of the gate. According to community wiki data, research progression is exclusively dedicated to unlocking new military buildings, infantry armaments, and missile technology. Crucially, higher-tier research nodes are gated behind your overall city development level.

To advance your technological capabilities, you must construct designated design bureaus known as Sloparms Offices. Upgrading these offices from small local workshops to massive engineering headquarters speeds up project completion and grants access to high-grade military hardware.

| Sloparms Tier | Typical Unlocks (Community Reports) | Requirements & Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Sloparms Office** | Baseline Infantry Rifles (e.g., M16 blueprints), Basic Munitions | Low city level; early game investment to prepare infantry defense |
| **Medium Sloparms Office** | Motorized transport, tactical drones, basic short-range ballistic modules | Mid-tier city levels; requires steady iron and refined fuel flow |
| **Large Sloparms Office** | Hypersonic tech, advanced anti-air batteries, heavy strategic launchers | Endgame metropolis status; requires large commercial revenue streams |

Remember that research solely unlocks the *ability* to produce or construct an item. You must still build dedicated manufacturing centers—such as the Military Factory or Large Military Factory—to fabricate physical units and ammunition stockpiles.

Modern confrontations in the game involve moving units through designated transit tunnels connecting sovereign player islands to the mainland contest zone. ## Air Defense Systems: Shielding Your Territory Against Bombardments

No offensive campaign can succeed if your home production grid is reduced to rubble while your troops are away. In Build and Conquer, opposing players frequently use high-yield tactical rockets and loitering drones to cripple competing economies. To prevent catastrophic power failures and collapsed supply chains, integrating advanced missile defense is an absolute priority.

When safeguarding your homeland and the staging zones for your **Build and Conquer army**, establish overlapping coverage zones using interceptor batteries. Placing multiple defensive installations along anticipated projectile trajectories ensures saturation attacks cannot penetrate your airspace.

| Defense System | Target Interception Profile | Recommended Role | Vulnerabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron Dome** | Short-range rockets, incoming mortar fire, light projectiles | Point defense over tightly packed commercial and industrial hubs | Can be overwhelmed by massed saturation barrages |
| **Patriot Battery** | Medium-range ballistic threats, high-altitude strikes | Area air defense covering residential and production sectors | High initial build cost, demands reliable power grid |
| **Patriot PAC-3** | Advanced tactical missiles, high-velocity warheads | High-priority asset defense (e.g., Sloparms offices, launch silos) | Requires continuous high-tier logistics and grid stability |

Position your interceptor networks close to power substations and major industrial corridors. If a stray strike eliminates your coal generator or refinery, the sudden power outage will shut down your anti-air radars, leaving your entire city vulnerable to follow-up salvos.

## Tactical Strategies for Mainland Dominance

Achieving victory on the competitive mainland requires more than just massing hardware. Player experience shows that coordinated operations, smart infrastructure redundancy, and disciplined logistics decide the outcome of modern conflicts. Follow these proven combat principles to keep your forces dominant:

1. **Build Redundant Power Grids:** Never rely entirely on a single coal plant or wind cluster. Distribute energy across your island with a mix of Wind Turbines, Solar Panels, and Coal Power Plants so a targeted strike does not black out your base.
2. **Fortify Tunnel Entrances:** Tunnels leading to the mainland are vital chokepoints. Station mechanized units and forward barracks near your exit portal to prevent hostile forces from bottling your army inside transit corridors.
3. **Form Alliances Early:** Fighting multiple hostile nations alone quickly depletes refined fuel and ammunition reserves. Coordinate defensive pacts with reliable neighbors to establish mutual air-defense umbrellas.
4. **Scout Before Launching Costly Missiles:** Short-Range Ballistic Missiles (SRBM) and Medium-Range Ballistic Missiles (MRBM) represent major economic investments. Confirm whether your target possesses active Patriot batteries before expending your high-end ordinance.
5. **Protect Fuel Refineries Above All Else:** Mechanized units, aircraft, and missile assembly lines require steady fuel. If your refineries fall, your offensive operations will immediately grind to a dead halt.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Build and Conquer Army

### How do I unlock weapons beyond the basic rifle for my Build and Conquer army?
Advanced weaponry, motorized equipment, and heavy ordinance are locked behind military research and city tier levels. Construct and upgrade Sloparms Offices while expanding your city's residential and commercial sectors. As your metropolis levels up, higher research nodes will become available to outfit your troops with superior gear.

### Why are my factories not producing military hardware or vehicle parts?
Military manufacturing requires a continuous flow of refined resources. If production stops, check whether your Mines and Oil Pumps have direct, uninterrupted road connectivity to your factories and refineries. Community reports indicate that even minor gaps in road tiles can halt delivery truck pathfinding, starving your military factories of essential inputs.

### What is the most effective way to protect my Build and Conquer army bases from missile barrages?
Install layered air defense networks featuring Iron Dome installations for short-range projectile swarms alongside Patriot Battery and Patriot PAC-3 platforms for high-velocity ballistic threats. Make sure all defense batteries are connected to redundant power grids so they remain operational during citywide blackouts.

### Can I relocate military buildings without losing resources?
In the current V0.1 Beta build, buildings cannot be freely moved across plots. Relocating structures generally requires demolishing the original building and reconstructing it on a new tile. Plan your base layout, road grids, and defensive perimeters carefully before committing heavy capital to advanced factories and launch pads.
