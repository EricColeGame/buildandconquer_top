## Unlocking Build and Conquer Units: City Milestones & Tech Trees

You cannot simply spawn combat divisions the moment you establish your country. Military infrastructure is strictly gated behind civilian milestones. The primary roadblock every new commander encounters is population density and worker allocation.

To access your first combat options, you must expand your residential neighborhoods until you reach **City Level 2**, which community wiki data verifies requires reaching a threshold of **250 citizens**. Upgrading to mid-rise apartments connected via two-lane paved roads with dedicated power and water utility networks is widely regarded by players as the most efficient way to cross this population mark quickly.

Once you reach City Level 2, the military development pipeline begins:

1. **Construct a Small Research Office (SLOPARMS):** Requires an investment of civic workers (often needing 15 available workers according to community reports) and continuous electrical power.
2. **Fund Weapons & Vehicle Research:** Projects like standard firearms, advanced optics, and vehicle blueprints require active funding cycles before they become buildable.
3. **Establish Heavy Industry:** Raw iron and crude oil must be extracted with mines and pumps, then refined into processed steel and fuel using factories and refineries.
4. **Deploy Assembly Facilities:** Build your Barracks, Military Car Storage, or Large Military Factory to transform stockpiled resources into combat-ready forces.

| City Level / Stage | Population Requirement | Unlocked Capabilities & Buildings | Community Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **City Level 1** | Plot Founding | Basic roads, housing, farms, power turbines, water towers | Foundation phase; no offensive military available |
| **City Level 2** | 250 Residents | Barracks, Small SLOPARMS Office, basic infantry weaponry | Essential early milestone; unlocks the military tree |
| **City Level 3** | Player Milestones | Advanced commercial buildings, light armor, drone workshops | Mid-game expansion; unlocks tactical map options |
| **City Level 4** | Advanced Civic Cap | Heavy industrial factories, advanced vehicle hulls, artillery | Transition to mechanized combined-arms warfare |
| **City Level 5** | Endgame Cap | High-tier missile launchers, top-tier interceptor batteries | Maximum recorded tier in current V0.1 beta testing |

*Note: Data above reflects community wiki data and active player observations. Exact unlock thresholds can change across future beta patches.*

## Infantry Squad Dynamics and Small Arms Gear

Infantry forms the foundational core of your armed services. While foot soldiers lack the raw armor plating of main battle tanks, they provide flexible, low-cost defensive screening for urban corridors and production hubs.

In the current V0.1 beta build, training infantry requires a functional Barracks. However, raw recruits cannot fight effectively without firearms produced through your industrial assembly line. Community reports note that weapons research takes place inside the SLOPARMS research office, beginning with foundational platforms like the M16 before branching into heavy firepower.

### Organizing Fireteams and Squadrons

Individual weapons are manufactured in your military factories using refined steel. Player reports indicate that foot soldiers operate under a squadron mechanic where **eight individual guns** are gathered to fully arm and deploy an operational infantry squadron. 

Equipping your front-line personnel with higher-tier research options increases their skirmishing effectiveness when contesting territory near transport tunnels.

| Weapon Platform | Research Tier / Origin | Primary Role | Strategic Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M16** | SLOPARMS Rank I | Basic Service Rifle | Standard issue for early-game defense squads |
| **Bloated M16** | SLOPARMS Rank II | Enhanced Infantry Rifle | Mid-tier upgrade offering increased firing power |
| **TRG-21 Interceptor** | Specialized Research | Precision Marksman Fire | Long-range harassment against advancing infantry |
| **Minimi** | Heavy Support Line | Squad Automatic Fire | Suppressive fire to stall approaching ground swarms |

Keeping your infantry supplied requires a stable cash flow. Because Barracks draw steady upkeep every 30-second financial cycle, avoid mass-producing troops until your commercial tax base—anchored by upgraded retail shops, supermarkets, and malls—is completely solvent.

## Armored Vehicles, Drones, and Artillery Lineup

Once your domestic infrastructure begins producing surplus steel and fuel, you can phase out light skirmishers in favor of mechanized **Build and Conquer units**. Heavy armor and automated strike craft dominate the open terrain of the mainland, making them essential for taking contested control zones.

Assembly takes place primarily in the **Large Military Factory** and specialized storage depots. Without steady deliveries of refined fuel from your refineries, mechanized divisions will stall out before reaching enemy lines.

```
[Raw Iron + Crude Oil]

[Mines & Refineries] ──► (Steel & Fuel) ──► [Large Military Factory] ──► Frontline Vehicles
```

### Mechanized and Air Support Roster

* **Mainline Tanks:** The spearhead for ground pushes. High durability allows them to absorb defensive point fire while cracking fortified perimeter structures.
* **T-72 & BMP-2 Chassis:** Featured in community reports via vehicle gamepass modules (such as the Eastern European equipment set), providing durable armored transports and cannons for contested zones.
* **Strike Drones (Shahed / Loitering Munitions):** Fast aerial units highlighted in recent beta updates. Drones can bypass ground barricades to strike utility lines and unprotected factories.
* **HIMARS Artillery Systems:** Long-range rocket artillery designed to bombard defensive encampments from well outside point-defense range. They excel at softening up player bases prior to a full armor push.

## Air Defense Networks and Anti-Missile Systems

Investing entirely in offensive armor will leave your homeland vulnerable if an opposing nation retaliates with high-altitude ordinance. Modern conflicts in Build and Conquer center heavily around layered air defense. If your industrial core is leveled by ballistic missiles, your frontline vehicle production immediately ceases.

A proper air defense umbrella uses multiple defensive **Build and Conquer units** to cover short, medium, and strategic ranges:

1. **Sea RAM:** A dedicated close-in weapon system. Community wiki data highlights that players are restricted to a **maximum placement limit of six Sea RAM units** per base, making placement around critical infrastructure essential.
2. **Iron Dome:** Designed to swat down incoming tactical artillery barrages and low-altitude strike munitions before they hit civilian districts.
3. **RA-3 Interceptor:** A premier late-game anti-air platform rated at the top of community tier lists for its reliability in neutralizing fast-moving aerial targets.
4. **Patriot Batteries (PAC-3):** Long-range missile interception units designed to counter high-altitude theater threats.

| Defense System | Target Classification | Placement Restriction | Optimal Tactical Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sea RAM** | Short-range point defense | Capped at 6 per base | Surround power plants and Large Military Factories |
| **Iron Dome** | Rocket barrages & light munitions | Standard grid placement | Position between your residential edge and the border |
| **Patriot PAC-3** | Theater ballistic threats | Standard grid placement | Deep interior placement behind civil defense sirens |
| **RA-3 Interceptor** | High-velocity targets & drones | Standard grid placement | High-tier perimeter coverage to protect air corridors |

Civil defense buildings, such as the Air Raid Siren and subterranean Bunkers, should be interspersed near residential sectors. While they do not destroy incoming warheads, community testing shows they significantly soften casualties and prevent population drops if an interceptor fails to engage.

## Strategic Ballistic Missiles and Mainland Combat

Ballistic weapons are the ultimate offensive **Build and Conquer units**. Unlike vehicles that navigate terrain, strategic missiles strike anywhere across the theater to eliminate industrial targets or neutralize hostile air defense batteries.

Missile technology follows a strict sequential progression. According to player experience reports, unlocking the next tier requires completing active combat tasks—specifically scoring verified hits against enemy structures or ground forces.

```
[SRBM Launcher] ──(100 Combat Hits)──► [GLCM] ──► [MRBM] ──► [Hypersonic BM]
```

### Strategic Missile Unlock Progression

* **SRBM (Short-Range Ballistic Missile):** The introductory tactical missile unlocked through the military tech tree. Relatively slow and vulnerable to standard interceptors, but serves as the necessary stepping stone.
* **GLCM (Ground-Launched Cruise Missile):** Offers a flatter trajectory and expanded operational range, requiring opponents to maintain active radar and low-altitude coverage.
* **MRBM (Medium-Range Ballistic Missile):** Delivers immense payload damage capable of leveling advanced factory complexes in a single strike.
* **Hypersonic BM:** The premier weapon of the V0.1 beta. Community reports confirm that unlocking the Hypersonic BM requires accumulating **100 successful hits on enemy targets** with earlier missiles. Its extreme velocity enables it to regularly bypass interceptor batteries.

| Missile Classification | Interception Difficulty | Splash Radius | Unlock Prerequisite |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SRBM** | High (Easily intercepted) | Moderate | Base military tech tree |
| **GLCM** | Moderate | Moderate-High | Combat progress with SRBM |
| **MRBM** | Low-Moderate | High | Combat progress with GLCM |
| **Hypersonic BM** | Extremely Low (High slip rate) | Massive | 100 verified hits on enemy assets |

### Mainland Deployment Tactics

Sending your forces to the contested mainland requires navigating them through dedicated transit tunnels. Entering the mainland automatically flags your nation for real-time PvP combat.

* **Capture Sector Nodes:** Capturing and holding strategic mainland sectors rewards you with an instant **+20% cash income boost** and a **+30% population capacity bonus**, supercharging your home economy.
* **Scout Before You Commit:** Avoid sending unsupported mainline tanks into narrow choke points. Probe enemy lines with cheap scout vehicles or HIMARS artillery to identify active Sea RAM and interceptor positions.
* **Coordinate Strikes:** Launch tactical cruise missiles immediately before a ground assault to saturate and distract the enemy's defensive missile batteries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best early-game Build and Conquer units to prioritize?
For early territorial defense and mainland skirmishes, prioritize unlocking the Barracks and researching the M16 at City Level 2. Once your economy stabilizes, transition into light strike drones and mainline tanks. Drones offer rapid response capabilities across rugged terrain, while tanks provide the durability needed to hold capture points.

### Why won't my military factories produce new units?
Vehicle and munitions assembly lines require steady resource logistics. If production halts, check your refinery output for processed steel and fuel deficits. Additionally, ensure your factories have adequate electrical power from your grid and sufficient unassigned workers from nearby residential apartments.

### How do you unlock the Hypersonic BM in Build and Conquer?
The Hypersonic BM is unlocked through an in-game combat milestone rather than passive research alone. According to player community reports, you must register 100 direct hits on enemy ground units or hostile base structures using earlier missile variants (such as the SRBM, GLCM, or MRBM).
