## Long-Range Offense: Ballistic Missiles and Strike Craft

Offensive military doctrine in the current meta revolves around long-range standoff bombardment. Long-range strikes allow you to target critical enemy nodes—such as refineries, power plants, and high-density commercial strips—without moving ground units through contested chokepoints.

```
[City Economy & Steel Mills] ──> [Sloparms Research] ──> [Missile Launch Complex] ──> [Enemy Base Target]
```

Among heavy **Build and Conquer weapons**, the Hypersonic Ballistic Missile (BM) represents the pinnacle of long-range destruction. Community reports highlight its steep trajectory and high terminal velocity, which render standard point-defense turrets virtually ineffective. However, firing these weapons consistently demands enormous amounts of refined steel, electricity, and industrial output.

For players seeking high efficiency without overextending their early economy, Medium-Range Ballistic Missiles (MRBMs) provide the best balance of range, damage, and affordability. Skipping Short-Range Ballistic Missiles (SRBMs) in favor of a direct tech rush toward MRBM launchers is a standard competitive opening observed in community play.

| Weapon Platform | Effective Combat Range | Interception Resistance | Economic Footprint | Ideal Combat Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hypersonic BM** | Extreme / Global | Very High | Heavy (Steel, High-Tier Tech) | Decapitation strikes on high-value industry |
| **MRBM** | Long Range | Moderate to High | Moderate (Standard Military Factory) | Primary bombardment of production districts |
| **GLCM** | Medium Range | Moderate | Moderate (Sloparms R&D required) | Tactical suppression of enemy perimeter guns |
| **SRBM** | Short Range | Low | Low (Early Sloparms / Barracks) | Early border skirmishes and deterrence |
| **Shahed Drone** | Variable / Standoff | Low to Moderate | Low (Resource-light assembly) | Swarm bait to exhaust enemy interceptor magazines |

Ground-launched cruise missiles (GLCM) and Shahed drones fill vital tactical niches. Drones in particular serve as economic sacrificial units. Launching a flight of low-cost drones seconds ahead of an MRBM or Hypersonic missile can bait enemy interceptors into firing early, depleting defensive reserves and clearing an unobstructed path for your heavy warhead.

## Tactical Combat Strategies and Weapon Combos

Victory against skilled opponents requires sophisticated tactical execution. Firing missiles the moment their cooldown timers reset rarely works against an established opponent with a functioning Iron Dome. To break through prepared defensive lines, you must coordinate launch timings across different weapon classifications.

```
Phase 1: Scout Base ──> Phase 2: Bait Defenses ──> Phase 3: Hypersonic Strike ──> Phase 4: Ground Exploitation
```

1. **Conduct Reconnaissance:** Deploy low-cost drones or fast vehicles to scout enemy anti-air placements and locate the enemy's electrical grid.
2. **Initiate Saturation Fire:** Launch a volley of Shahed loitering munitions or low-cost rockets. This forces enemy Iron Dome batteries and automated turrets to expend their active magazines.
3. **Execute Precision Decapitation:** Once defense systems are reloading or distracted, launch your Hypersonic BM or MRBM payload directly at the opponent's primary power generation plants or water supplies.
4. **Deploy Ground Exploitation:** Move mainline tanks and motorized infantry through underground tunnels or perimeter roads to clear remaining perimeter defenses and capture contested ground.

| Deployment Phase | Primary Weapon Systems | Primary Objective | Community Execution Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Game (Level 1–2)** | Barracks, Light Vehicles, Basic SRBM | Defend perimeter; claim resource nodes | Avoid heavy missile spam; prioritize commercial cash flow |
| **Mid Game (Level 3–4)** | MRBMs, GLCMs, Iron Dome, Sea RAM | Establish regional dominance; suppress raids | Build overlapping air defense around refineries |
| **Late Game (Level 5+)** | Hypersonic BMs, Patriot PAC-3, Drones | Decapitate rival bases; break defense grids | Fire synchronized drone-and-ballistic salvos |

By executing this layered attack pattern, you maximize the impact of your most expensive munitions while minimizing waste against heavily defended targets.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Build and Conquer weapons in the current meta?
According to player experience and creator testing during the V0.1 beta, the Hypersonic BM is widely considered the top offensive weapon due to its rapid flight profile and resilience against interception. For air defense, combining an Iron Dome with the maximum allowance of six Sea RAM installations offers the most reliable protection for your city.

### Why is the Sea RAM point-defense system capped at six per base?
Community reports indicate that developer The Slop Company implemented a six-unit cap on Sea RAM emplacements to prevent players from creating impenetrable point-defense bubbles. This hard limit requires players to make strategic choices about which infrastructure nodes—such as power generators and high-tier military factories—deserve direct close-range coverage.

### How do you unlock advanced military weapons in Build and Conquer?
High-tier weaponry is locked behind city level thresholds and dedicated military research. You must upgrade your city by expanding residential neighborhoods, commercial centers, and public utilities. Once eligibility requirements are met, constructing Small, Medium, or Large Sloparms Offices allows you to research blueprints for weapons like the MRBM and Hypersonic BM.

### Should beginners research SRBMs or save for MRBMs?
Community testing suggests that while the SRBM is a cheap early deterrence weapon, it is easily tracked and neutralized by even entry-level interceptor networks. Players seeking maximum economic efficiency often build basic ground defenses for early protection, saving their research capital and steel reserves to rush directly into MRBM and GLCM technology.
