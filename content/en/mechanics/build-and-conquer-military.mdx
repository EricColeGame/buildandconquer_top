## The Industrial Foundation: Economy-to-War Pipeline

In this title, you cannot simply buy pre-assembled tanks and missile launchers out of thin air. Every weapon system, interceptor battery, and combat chassis must be researched, funded, powered, and manufactured inside your borders. A military without a robust industrial backbone will stall before deploying its first patrol.

Your nation runs on three foundational civilian pillars: **power grids**, **water networks**, and **residential housing**. Houses and apartment complexes generate the workforce needed to operate factories. Commercial storefronts like shops, strip malls, and supermarkets provide the tax income necessary to bankroll war efforts.

Once civilian basics are stable, heavy resource extraction feeds your military-industrial complex. Mines extract iron and coal, while oil pumps draw crude reserves. Refineries then process these raw materials into refined fuel and industrial metal. Without an uninterrupted delivery line of steel and fuel, advanced assembly buildings cannot construct mechanized units or munitions.

| Resource / Facility | Primary Input | Operational Output | Military Function | Supply Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coal & Iron Mines** | Raw Map Deposits | Raw Coal, Iron Ore | Feeds industrial smelters and factory floors | High (Early Game) |
| **Oil Pump & Refineries** | Raw Crude Oil | Refined Fuel, Petrochemicals | Supplies vehicle engines and missile propellants | Critical (Mid-to-Late Game) |
| **Small / Medium Factory**| Processed Metals | Consumer Goods / Parts | Bridges initial production needs | Medium |
| **Water & Power Plants** | Fuel / Wind / Solar / Coal | Grid Power & Running Water | Keeps manufacturing facilities active | Vital (Non-negotiable) |

If your power drops or your citizens run low on water, factories halt immediately. Experienced players recommend creating a designated industrial corridor situated well away from vulnerable border plots to safeguard critical refining plants.

## Unlocking and Advancing Military Research

Technological progression determines the sophistication of your armed forces. You cannot field advanced missile complexes immediately upon claiming a plot.

According to community wiki data and gameplay observations, research milestones are tied directly to city expansion. Early military capabilities typically unlock once your municipal population hits verified thresholds, such as reaching 250 residents to access Level 2 town status. Rushing population density using mid-rise apartments early on is the fastest route toward clearing these initial military hurdles.

```
[Raw Population Growth] ──> [City Level Upgrades] ──> [Sloparms Research Offices] ──> [Military Factories & Launchers]
```

Research facilities operate through corporate defense infrastructure, primarily via Sloparms structures:

*   **Small Sloparms Office:** Unlocks entry-level military blueprints, basic logistics, and standard garrison options.
*   **Medium Sloparms Office:** Expands the tech tree into specialized armored vehicle chassis and intermediate interceptor nodes.
*   **Large Sloparms Office:** Unlocks high-tier missile systems, advanced guidance packages, and heavy strike platforms.

| Facility Type | Category | Community-Reported Tech Unlocks | Strategic Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barracks** | Personnel / Infantry | Basic ground personnel and security crews | Base defense and municipal policing |
| **Small / Medium Sloparms Office** | Technology / Research | Foundational military items, vehicle schematics | Expands early-to-mid tier blueprints |
| **Large Sloparms Office** | High-Tier Research | Advanced rocketry, ballistic calculations | Enables late-game deterrent weapons |
| **Military Factory** | Manufacturing | Standard armored transport and basic munitions | Assembles low-tier combat hardware |
| **Large Military Factory** | Heavy Manufacturing | Main battle platforms, cruise munitions, strike drones | Mass-produces heavy mainland strike assets |

Research exclusively unlocks new military items and structures. If you find your tech tree stalled, check your population census, commercial revenue, and city level before troubleshooting your research offices.

## Structuring the Build and Conquer Military Defense Grid

Neglecting air defense is the most common reason beginner cities are reduced to rubble. Hostile players on neighboring parcels can launch drone strikes or ballistic payloads directly toward your commercial centers, crippling your revenue within seconds.

Building a resilient **Build and Conquer military** defense requires overlapping fields of fire. Relying on a single interceptor model leaves blind spots against fast-moving hypersonic weapons or saturation drone swarms.

### Point Defense vs. Area Denial

1.  **Close-In Point Defense (Sea RAM):** Point-defense installations target incoming projectiles that slip past long-range shields. Community reports highlight a strict placement limit (often around six Sea RAM units per base), making their positioning around your central town hall and power plants vital.
2.  **Short-to-Medium Interceptors (Iron Dome):** Highly effective against low-altitude drone strikes and standard artillery munitions. They fire rapidly to clear incoming saturation barrages.
3.  **Long-Range Air Defense (Patriot Battery / Patriot PAC-3):** These high-altitude batteries engage long-range ballistic rockets well before they descend onto your skyline.

| Defensive System | Weapon Classification | Optimal Tactical Target | Placement Limitation (Community Reported) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sea RAM** | Automated Close-In Weapon System (CIWS) | Leakers, close rockets, incoming cruise munitions | Hard cap (reported ~6 per plot) |
| **Iron Dome** | Tactical Air Defense Battery | Drone swarms, low-altitude rockets | Medium footprint, high ammunition demand |
| **Patriot Battery** | High-Altitude Air Defense | Medium-range ballistic missiles (MRBMs) | Large power grid load |
| **Patriot PAC-3** | Advanced Interceptor Battery | High-speed ballistic weapons and heavy ordnance | High tech-tier research requirement |

To maximize defensive coverage, position long-range batteries toward the outward edges of your base facing the mainland, while clustering point-defense batteries tightly around essential refineries and electrical hubs.

## Offensive Hardware: Drones, Armor, and Missiles

Once your borders are secured, offensive firepower allows you to project power across the server. Building a dominant offensive **Build and Conquer military** branch involves three key pillars: drone warfare, mobile ground units, and precision missile launchers.

Assembly takes place primarily inside your Military Factory and Large Military Factory structures. These facilities pull steel, refined fuel, and research clearances to assemble strike assets.

### The Missile Escalation Ladder

Community guides note that ballistic capabilities follow a sequential combat progression. Rather than simply researching every rocket with cash, top-tier hypersonic capabilities require operational combat experience. Community reports indicate that players must register confirmed hits on hostile targets using earlier short-range missile models to unlock advanced hypersonic systems.

```
[SRBM Launcher] ──> (Register Target Hits) ──> [MRBM Launcher] ──> (Achieve Combat Milestones) ──> [Hypersonic BM]
```

| Strike Asset | Community Category | Primary Mission Profile | Logistical Requirement |

Keep in mind that firing high-tier missiles consumes substantial quantities of processed munitions and fuel. Always check your warehouse levels before initiating an extended artillery or rocket bombardment.

## Mainland Warfare and Strategic Sector Conquest

Every nation's plot is separated from the common warzone by subterranean transit paths. Deploying your **Build and Conquer military** vehicles through these transport tunnels takes your forces directly onto the shared mainland.

Moving units into mainland territory automatically flags your nation for real-time player-versus-player combat. The mainland contains neutral and contested control zones. Holding these key points offers immense economic and municipal rewards that accelerate your city's development.

Player experience and community documentation report significant territorial perks for capturing strategic sectors:
*   **Income Multipliers:** Occupying vital mainland objectives grants substantial income boosts (frequently reported at a 20% tax revenue boost).
*   **Population Capacity Expansion:** Strategic hubs can provide up to a 30% bonus to residential capacity, allowing for faster city leveling.
*   **Allied Coordination:** Servers support alliances of up to 8 players, allowing coalition partners to build mutual air defense umbrellas over captured control points.

| Conquest Phase | Tactical Objective | Operational Risk | Reported Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Tunnel Transit** | Move mechanized armor safely to mainland staging zones | Bottleneck ambush at tunnel exits | Secure entry point onto mainland |
| **Phase 2: Sector Capture** | Clear hostile garrisons and establish perimeter presence | Counter-battery fire from rival launch sites | Instant income and population cap bonuses |
| **Phase 3: Fortification** | Construct forward interceptor shields and fuel caches | Sustained hypersonic bombardment | Long-term mainland dominance and resource control |

Coordinate deployments with server allies whenever possible. Sending unescorted transport trucks onto the mainland makes them easy prey for loitering drones. Always maintain radar awareness and establish overlapping anti-air shields as your ground forces advance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I unlock my first Build and Conquer military units?
Unlocking foundational military equipment requires expanding your residential city first. Community reports indicate that reaching approximately 250 citizens unlocks City Level 2, which grants access to the Barracks, early Sloparms research, and entry-level factory production.

### What is the most effective defense against incoming hypersonic missiles?
Defeating hypersonic threats requires a layered air defense grid. High-altitude Patriot PAC-3 interceptors thin out the incoming volley at long distance, while localized point-defense batteries like the Sea RAM engage any leakers right before impact.

### Why did my military vehicle assembly stop suddenly?
Vehicle assembly halts when underlying supply chains break down. The most common causes are fuel shortages from disconnected refineries, lack of power grid output, missing road connectivity, or factory worker deficits caused by low residential happiness.

### How do alliances work during mainland combat?
Players can form diplomatic pacts with other commanders on the server. Allied forces can coordinate simultaneous pushes through transport tunnels, share defensive perimeters, and protect captured mainland control sectors to maintain shared territory buffs.
