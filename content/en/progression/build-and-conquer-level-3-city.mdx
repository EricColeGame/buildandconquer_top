## Step-by-Step Path to Reach Level 3

Scaling your population efficiently is the cornerstone of establishing a **Build and Conquer level 3 city**. In the early stages of Level 2, many players hit an expansion plateau where building single-family homes consumes too much physical plot real estate without generating enough population density to trigger the upgrade.

### 1. Optimize Residential Density
Relying solely on Level 1 houses like the Poor House (+3 Pop) or standard Residential Houses (+5 Pop) quickly chokes your available building borders. To push toward Level 3, phase out low-density zoning in favor of Level 2 apartments. The Small Suburban Apartment (+24 Pop) and Large Suburban Apartment (+40 Pop) drastically compress your urban footprint while adding positive citizen happiness.

### 2. Safeguard Citizen Happiness
Happiness directly impacts settlement productivity. Communal Housing 1 provides a rapid influx of +44 population, but community wiki data notes it inflicts a -14 happiness penalty on residents. If happiness crashes, economic output suffers. Counteract high-density tenements with Large Suburban Apartments (+3 happiness) and surrounding retail shops.

### 3. Balance the Labor Market
Every commercial office and utility structure requires active workers. If your population is too small, factories and power stations will face severe labor shortages, cutting off municipal services.

## Unlocks and Infrastructure in a Level 3 City

Crossing the threshold into a **Build and Conquer level 3 city** transforms your building menu by introducing an essential resource: **Steel**. Prior to Level 3, the vast majority of civilian and commercial assets require only flat cash reserves, electricity, and water. At Level 3, structural urbanization demands industrial inputs.

### The Arrival of Steel
Whether you plan to zone high-yield retail or construct regional safety infrastructure, steel becomes non-negotiable. For instance, constructing a single Municipal Precinct requires 200 units of Steel alongside $20,000 cash. You must ensure your factory districts and resource supply lines are operating before upgrading your skyline.

### Key Level 3 Unlocked Structures

1. **City Apartment**: The first high-density urban apartment building. It houses +20 population, generates +$80 per pay cycle, and awards +4 happiness, but requires 22 Steel, 15 Energy, and -$12 per pay cycle in upkeep.
2. **Municipal Precinct**: The premier civic safety building for mid-game administration. While demanding 200 Steel and a hefty -$1,000 maintenance per pay cycle, it provides the backbone for city stability.
3. **Large Supermarket**: A major commercial earner providing +$260 per pay cycle and +13 resident happiness, serving up to 100 citizens.
4. **Low Rise Office Building**: Unlocks white-collar commercial revenue of +$420 per cycle, employing 30 workers and requiring 70 Steel.
5. **Big Gas Station**: Expands commercial logistics, providing +$175 per pay cycle to serve 70 citizens.

## Utility Grid and Resource Management

Powering and sustaining a **Build and Conquer level 3 city** requires careful grid management. You can no longer rely on standalone solar panels and scattered wind turbines to power heavy commercial towers and municipal facilities.

### Power Strategy: Transitioning to Coal
At Level 1, Solar Panels provide 20 Energy and Wind Turbines generate 200 Energy. However, mid-tier commercial complexes and municipal precincts rapidly deplete small green grids. 

The Coal Power Plant, unlocked at Level 2, is the workhorse of your Level 3 expansion. It produces 6,000 Energy and stores up to 1,000 units of Coal for an upkeep of -$18 per pay cycle. Ensure your road networks allow steady resource hauling to prevent sudden brownouts.

### Water Distribution
Water coverage is mandatory for residential occupancy. The starter Water Tower serves up to 200 citizens within a 500x500 stud radius for -$2 per pay cycle while adding +12 happiness. However, once your population surges past several hundred residents, individual towers become inefficient. 

The Desalination Plant provides city-wide unlimited water distribution for $5,000, but players must note its scaling drawback: energy consumption increases by +1,000 per citizen. If your power grid is unstable, a Desalination Plant can trigger cascading blackouts across your entire district.

Maintaining positive cash flow while upgrading to a **Build and Conquer level 3 city** requires zoning discipline. Because servers accommodate up to 8 competing players, plot vulnerability increases as your settlement grows in value and visibility.

### Zoning Guidelines
- **Separate Industrial from Residential**: Keep heavy logistics, coal drop-offs, and future missile production away from high-density housing blocks to optimize traffic flow for trucks.
- **Buffer Utilities with Commercial Belts**: Place retail establishments, like Large Supermarkets and Strip Malls, between your residential neighborhoods and commercial office blocks. This ensures residents quickly fulfill commercial needs to keep happiness elevated.
- **Protect Civic Infrastructure**: Place your Municipal Precinct and power hubs toward the rear or center of your plot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why am I stuck at Level 2 instead of unlocking a Build and Conquer level 3 city?
Achieving a **Build and Conquer level 3 city** requires meeting both population thresholds and active utility coverage. If your houses lack sufficient water or electricity, residents will not count toward functional progression requirements. Additionally, check your employment rates; having empty commercial buildings without sufficient workers can stall urban growth.

### Where do I get Steel for Level 3 construction?
Steel is manufactured through industrial processing plants by combining raw resources like iron and coal. Before placing down Level 3 structures like City Apartments or the Municipal Precinct, ensure your logistics network of supply trucks is delivering raw materials to your factories.

### Are there active promo codes to skip early progression?
As of current Beta checks, there are no active promotional redeem codes officially released by The Slop Company. Be wary of unauthorized third-party sites promising free cash codes or instant level skips; all valid updates are announced through official community channels.

### Do I need to build a Desalination Plant to reach Level 3?
No, a Desalination Plant is not mandatory. You can sustain a Level 3 population using strategically placed Water Towers across your residential neighborhoods. While Water Towers require more physical space, they avoid the extreme scaling energy consumption (+1,000 Energy per citizen) associated with the Desalination Plant.
