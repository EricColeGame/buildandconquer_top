## How Progression and City Tiers Function

City progression in Build and Conquer revolves around population growth. Rather than simply spending money to upgrade your main base, community wiki data notes that city level increases as set conditions are met, and these conditions are consistently population-oriented. 

As your population grows, your settlement hits predefined citizen targets. Meeting these requirements triggers an upgrade to the next tier, unlocking modern residential apartments, commercial retail hubs, high-capacity energy generators, and advanced municipal offices. 

According to player experience and active community testing, advancing through higher tiers also exposes players to heavier economic demands. Higher-density buildings require refined industrial materials like steel, massive supplies of continuous energy, and steady municipal wages.

| City Level Tier | Primary Focus | Key Housing Unlocked | Key Utility & Service Unlocks | Upkeep & Resource Demands |

| **Level 1** | Settlement Founding | Houses, Suburban Houses, Two Story Houses | Solar Panels, Wind Turbines, Water Towers | Basic cash flow, zero raw materials required |
| **Level 2** | Suburban Expansion | Suburban Apartments, Communal Housing | Coal Power Plants, Sheriff's Office, Banks | Continuous coal supply, citizen happiness tracking |

| **Level 5+** | Superpower Endgame | Late-game residential blocks | Advanced national defense & high-output networks | Massive multi-resource supply chains |

*Note: Data above reflects community reports and player testing from V0.1 Beta builds. Future updates may adjust specific level gates.*

## Strategies to Level Up Your City Fast

Optimizing your layout for rapid advancement across Build and Conquer city levels requires balancing citizen satisfaction against resource logistics. Using these proven development principles will keep your city climbing the ranks efficiently:

1.  **Prioritize Water Coverage Density:** Early on, Water Towers provide cheap coverage within a 500x500 stud box. Group residential housing tightly inside this perimeter before building on unserviced plots.
2.  **Avoid Communal Housing Happiness Traps:** While Communal Housing 1 provides +44 population cheaply at Level 2, its harsh -14 happiness penalty can drag your settlement's overall growth down. Counterbalance it with Strip Malls (+10 happiness) and Supermarkets (+13 happiness).
3.  **Stockpile Coal Before Upgrading Power:** Never commission a Coal Power Plant until your supply depots have stable coal reserves. If your plant runs out of coal, citywide power collapses instantly.
4.  **Balance Commercial Office Ratios:** Large corporate offices generate thousands of dollars per pay cycle, but they absorb dozens of workers. Keep an eye on your available labor pool so your military bases and power plants don't face worker shortages.
5.  **Prepare Smelters Before Level 3:** Build iron extraction sites and steel refineries during Level 2 so you immediately have the raw steel needed for Level 3 and Level 4 high-rises.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What determines upgrades between Build and Conquer city levels?
Upgrades between city levels are governed strictly by your overall population count and meeting baseline municipal conditions. As your total population reaches set development milestones, your city tier increases, granting access to more advanced blueprints in the construction menu.

### Why is my settlement not advancing to the next city level?
If your population is high but your city level has not increased, confirm that your residential structures have unbroken access to both power and water. Abandoned or unserviced houses do not contribute properly to your active population thresholds. Check your status menus for unmet civic requirements.

### Which buildings provide the fastest population growth in early city levels?
In Level 1, Two Story Suburban Houses offer the most space-efficient population (+8 Pop for $176). Once you unlock Level 2, Small Suburban Apartments (+24 Pop) and Large Suburban Apartments (+40 Pop) provide the safest, happiness-positive population surges to push you toward higher city tiers.
