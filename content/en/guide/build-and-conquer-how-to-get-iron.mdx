## Build and Conquer How to Get Iron: The Mine Building Step-by-Step

When investigating **Build and Conquer how to get iron**, the direct answer lies in the **Mine** structure situated within the Resources building category. However, players mapping out **Build and Conquer how to get iron** must understand the strict resource dependencies required before ground excavation begins. A Mine placed in isolation without workers or electricity will sit completely dormant, draining your treasury without yielding a single ingot.

Follow these practical steps to establish your first functional iron extraction node:

1.  **Zone a Dedicated Industrial Grid:** Keep heavy extraction nodes physically separated from high-density civilian residences to prevent layout conflicts and maintain efficient road corridors.
2.  **Verify Surplus Power Capacity:** Ensure your regional power grid has active reserve wattage. An under-powered mine produces zero ore and can trigger cascading brownouts across adjacent utility lines.

4.  **Place the Mine Facility:** Open your construction catalog, navigate to the **Resources** tab, select the **Mine**, and deploy it onto your cleared industrial parcel.
5.  **Connect Transportation Links:** Lay road corridors connecting your extraction sector directly toward industrial processing zones and military research sectors.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Iron Production

### Why is my extraction halted when following guides on Build and Conquer how to get iron?
If your Mine is placed but producing nothing, the problem is almost always an unfulfilled utility or workforce requirement. Check your municipal status bar to ensure your electrical grid has surplus wattage and your residential zones are providing unassigned workers. If water supply issues force citizens out of nearby housing, the resulting labor shortage will shut down operations across your industrial mines.

### Can I purchase iron or steel directly with cash from the building menu?
No. In *Build and Conquer*, heavy industrial metals cannot be purchased directly through a storefront menu. You must extract iron using domestic Resource facilities like the Mine and process it through your industrial manufacturing infrastructure. This makes resource planning an essential mechanic in the game.

### At what city level should I prioritize iron extraction?
Community players recommend securing basic civilian stability during Level 1 and early Level 2 before heavily investing in iron extraction. Ensure you have sustainable tax revenue from shops, stable water coverage, and green power from wind turbines first. Once you approach Level 3—where major structures like City Apartments and Low Rise Offices demand raw steel—you should bring your iron mines online immediately.

### Are iron deposits randomized across the map plot?
In current beta versions of *Build and Conquer*, resource construction is managed through plot zoning. Players place resource extraction facilities directly within their unlocked territory rather than scouting out hidden procedural mineral nodes. Keep in mind that developer updates during the V0.1 Beta may alter map layouts and resource distribution over time.
