## The Core Economy: How Pay Cycles and Cash Flow Work

Wealth generation in the game does not follow a typical clicker model where coins drop continuously into your bank. Instead, the economy operates on a fixed **30-second pay cycle**. Every half-minute, the municipal engine tallies your city's total revenue, deducts the operating costs of all active buildings, and deposits the net surplus directly into your balance.

Understanding this 30-second loop is vital. If your expenses exceed your gross earnings when the timer strikes zero, your account suffers an immediate net loss. Sustained negative cycles will rapidly deplete your starting funds, locking you out of vital emergency purchases or upgrades.

```
Net Pay Cycle Profit = (Commercial Revenue + Resident Tax Revenue) - (Building Upkeep + Utility Running Costs + Military Payroll)
```

Gross income primarily stems from two civilian pillars: resident taxes collected from residential housing and business revenue generated by retail stores and office complexes. Conversely, expenses stem from road placement, utility maintenance (such as wind turbines, coal power, or water towers), and public security facilities.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial Revenue** | Income | Shops, Gas Stations, Strip Malls, Banks, Offices | Primary profit engine; staff with adequate workers |
| **Resident Taxes** | Income | Houses, Apartments, High-Rise Residential | Secondary income; provides the workforce for shops |
| **Utility Funding** | Expense | Power plants, Water Towers, Desalination plants | Keep supply slightly above current population demand |
| **Civic Services** | Expense | Sheriff's Offices, Precincts, Fire/Emergency shelters | Place only when city tier mandates or crime rises |
| **Military Upkeep** | Expense | Barracks, Research Labs, Defense batteries, Munitions | Delay heavy military spending until civilian cash is secure |

## Early Game Cash Setup: Your First 10 Minutes

When you spawn onto an allocated server plot marked by a glowing border, you start with exactly **$2,500** in starting capital. While that amount feels generous, haphazardly dropping expensive structures can wipe out your budget in seconds. Fortunately, new players receive several critical free starter assets to jumpstart development.

According to player experience and early city tutorials, your first water tower, first wind turbine, first barracks, and first research station do not cost building capital. Deploying these complimentary units early prevents you from wasting your $2,500 budget on infrastructure before laying down cash-generating assets.

### 1. Establish the Road Backbone
Every functioning structure must sit within **4 studs** of a connected roadway. If a building is too far from a street, a red outline appears, signaling an invalid placement that cannot receive power, water, or incoming workers. Standard two-lane asphalt roads cost **$10 per tile**, but dirt roads are completely free. Lay down dirt roads initially to map out residential and commercial zones, preserving your liquid cash for actual revenue generators.

### 2. Connect Basic Utilities
Place your free wind turbine and free water tower along your main avenue. Keep in mind that power and water networks do not cross through conflicting utility structures. Make sure both lines have clear coverage over the plots where your initial citizens and retailers will operate.

### 3. Deploy Starter Housing and Your First Shop
Once power and water are established, drop starter houses to attract your initial citizens. Citizens generate modest tax income, but more importantly, they provide the workforce required to operate retail storefronts. As soon as you have two available workers, construct your first **Shop**.

A basic Shop costs approximately $400, requires two workers, and generates a healthy profit each pay cycle. Once built, open its interaction menu and purchase the **Level 2 Shop Upgrade** for $200. This upgrade significantly enhances cycle earnings, offering one of the fastest return-on-investment plays in the opening minutes of the experience.

## Residential Planning and Workforce Balancing

A common trap for novice city planners is expanding residential zones indiscriminately. If you place rows of houses without matching commercial employment, your city collects minimal residential taxes while absorbing heavy utility maintenance costs.

In the game's simulation model, citizens belong to socioeconomic strata. Residents seek employment matching their class and prefer patronizing commercial properties tailored to their demographic. Furthermore, commercial buildings will not produce a single dollar if they lack available workers.

### Population Density Milestones
To unlock higher-tier facilities—such as the Small Sloparms Research Office, which requires **15 available workers**—relying purely on low-density single-family homes is too slow and consumes excessive plot real estate. Instead, transition toward high-density apartments as soon as your player level permits.

## Pro Strategies to Avoid Bankruptcy and Maximize Profits

Maintaining an ironclad economy in the experience requires balancing growth with fiscal restraint. Many players stumble into a deficit loop where construction costs drain their wallet to zero, leaving them unable to repair broken grids or build additional power plants. 

Apply these battle-tested strategies to safeguard your income stream:

### 1. Delay the Military Industrial Complex
Military strength is the end goal, but army infrastructure generates zero direct profit. Barracks, drone hangars, missile launchers, and heavy munitions factories carry punishing pay-cycle upkeeps. Placing a barracks or armory before establishing a $300+/cycle surplus can quickly throttle your growth. Establish a booming civilian commercial sector before funding military research or recruit training.

### 2. Manage the Commercial Happiness Radius
Commercial structures influence neighborhood satisfaction. For example, a basic Shop provides **+2 happiness** to residences within 40 studs, but imposes a **-2 noise penalty** on homes within 20 studs. Keep a buffer of at least 20 to 25 studs between heavy retail hubs and residential zones. Satisfied citizens pay full tax rates and ensure stable municipal operations.

### 3. Upgrade Existing Assets Before New Construction
Before expanding your perimeter and purchasing expensive new utility lines, audit your current footprint. Upgrading existing shops, gas stations, and utilities increases income density per stud without incurring additional land clearance or road upkeep costs.

### 4. Build Ahead of Your Power Grid
Power outages instantly shut down commercial enterprises. When energy hits zero, businesses stop producing income, but their base operational debts persist. Always build a secondary wind turbine or upgrade toward a Coal Power Plant ($5,000, provides 6,000 Energy) before placing high-draw commercial complexes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use promo codes in Build and Conquer to get quick cash?
No. There are currently no official redemption codes available for the game. Although a redemption box may appear within certain menus, The Slop Company has not released any public currency codes. Any third-party websites offering codes for instant funds are referring to unrelated titles or circulating false strings. Always check official community announcements for legitimate promotional updates.

### Why am I losing money on every pay cycle?
If your bank balance drops every 30 seconds, your operational expenses exceed your gross earnings. This typically happens when players construct too many military buildings, overbuild safety services (such as expensive precincts or sheriff stations), or lay commercial buildings that lack the necessary workers to function. Deconstruct redundant public services and ensure every shop has adequate labor to restore positive cash flow.

### In Build and Conquer how to get money when completely broke?
If your treasury hits zero and your net income is negative, pause all new construction. Sell off non-essential military assets or high-maintenance municipal buildings via their individual interaction menus to recoup capital. Replace paid roads with free dirt roads, verify that all power plants have fuel, and inspect commercial buildings to confirm they are staffed and connected within 4 studs of an active street.

### Does citizen happiness impact how much money I earn?
Yes. Community testing indicates that happy citizens maintain higher productivity and pay their full tax allotments each cycle. Severe happiness penalties—caused by placing heavy industrial factories, noise-generating storefronts, or polluters too close to residential housing—can depress worker efficiency and reduce overall municipal tax collections. Always maintain appropriate zoning separation between industrial plants and residential suburbs.
