## Getting Started: Founding Your Nation and Laying Roads

When you load into a server, the game allocates you an empty plot bordered by a bright cyan boundary line. You receive a starting balance of $2,500. While that bankroll feels generous, reckless purchasing can drain your funds in under two minutes. 

First, open the nation establishment interface. Input your country name, select your flag, and confirm your claim to lock in your territory. From there, open the construction menu (the hammer icon) and navigate to the Roads tab.

Roads form the circulatory system of your entire settlement. Every single utility, home, store, and production plant must sit within four studs of an active road, or it will refuse to function. While standard two-lane roads cost $10 per unit, dirt roads are completely free. Smart planners rough out their entire zone layout with dirt paths first to map block dimensions before committing cash to paved streets.

## Essential Infrastructure: Power, Water, and Housing

Before placing residential zones, you must establish baseline utilities. The game grants every player one free wind turbine and one free water tower. Open your Services menu and place these directly beside your main road. 

Water and electricity in Build and Conquer operate via direct proximity networks. Power cannot flow through water pipes, and water cannot pass through electrical substations. If you place a power building separated from a shop by an unrelated water tank, your circuit breaks. Keep your service corridors neatly organized along parallel street lines to avoid connectivity dead ends.

When you select a building blueprint, watch the ghost preview outline:
* **Green Outline:** Valid site. The structure is within four studs of a functional road and has clear utility pathways.
* **Red Outline:** Blocked placement. The structure is either detached from the road network, lacks power or water capacity, or collides with existing terrain.

## Establishing a Stable Economy: Income Cycles and Commercial Scaling

Build and Conquer runs on an automated tax and revenue cycle: net profits are calculated and distributed once every 30 seconds. This payout compiles your business sales, residential rents, and production surplus, then subtracts municipal upkeep and power maintenance. 

To turn a profit, open the Commercial menu and build a Shop along your primary corridor. A standard shop requires two active workers to open its doors. Citizens will only take jobs matching their socioeconomic tier, meaning low-density homes supply standard storefront clerks, while high-tier apartments support commercial offices.

Commercial happiness mechanics also play a major role in your income:
* **Proximity Boost:** Homes located between 21 and 40 studs away from a shop receive a +2 happiness bonus.
* **Noise Penalty:** Homes built directly adjacent (within 20 studs) suffer a -2 happiness penalty due to foot traffic.

To maximize income, leave a small green belt or single-tile gap between your commercial centers and your sleeping residential quarters.

Once your shop is built, click it and invest $200 in the Level 2 upgrade. Upgrades multiply earnings per cycle without introducing additional utility drains, giving you an immediate boost in net profit.

Don't ignore agriculture, either. A basic Farm Field costs $100 and requires 1 worker. Remember that Farm Fields must touch an existing Wheat Farm or Farm Field tile to operate. Any surplus food your citizens don't consume is automatically sold during your 30-second financial tally, generating extra revenue.

## Technology Tree and SLOPARMS Military Research

Once your residential foundation produces steady cash, you must transition toward technology and defense. The core of your strategic progression is the Small Research Office, which costs $400, drains 10 energy and 8 water, and carries a $10 per-cycle upkeep fee.

However, placing this building requires a milestone that catches many new players off guard: you must have **15 available, unemployed workers** before the game allows placement.

Our **Build and Conquer starters guide** recommends focusing on worker density to clear this hurdle quickly. Building five individual suburban houses costs $500 and eats up valuable road frontage. Instead, save for a Middle Class Apartment 1/4 ($450 community cost), which delivers +18 residents in a compact footprint.

After staffing the Small Research Office, select it to view the SLOPARMS technology tree. Only one project can be researched at a time. Highlight the **M16** under Infantry Rank I and begin research. You can contribute up to $1,000 to accelerate the five-minute timer. 

A common beginner mistake is assuming research instantly spawns weaponry. Research simply unlocks the blueprint. To physically equip your troops, you must construct a Military Factory to manufacture the rifles using extracted raw materials.

## Resource Extraction, Manufacturing, and Base Defense

Transitioning from a peaceful town to a regional military power requires mining and heavy industry. While commercial buildings generate tax income, military production runs on three raw materials: Coal, Iron, and Oil.

Construct Mines over mineral deposits to produce Coal (for power) and Iron (for manufacturing). Build Oil Pumps to feed Small Refineries, which convert crude reserves into vehicle fuel and military-grade synthetics.

| Industrial Facility | Raw Input Required | Processed Output | Infrastructure Demand | Strategic Importance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mine** | Mineral Deposit Node | Raw Iron & Coal | Road connection + heavy power | Feeds factories and heavy power grids. |
| **Oil Pump** | Oil Reserve Deposit | Crude Oil | Pipeline / Road Access | Supplies domestic refineries. |
| **Small Refinery** | Crude Oil | Usable Fuel & Polymers | Road connection + water line | Keeps mechanized vehicles rolling. |
| **Military Factory** | Processed Iron & Tech | Field Weapons (M16s, etc.)| High electricity + workforce | Assembles weapons researched at SLOPARMS. |
| **Barracks** | $1,000 / Free First Unit| Trained Infantry Units | 2 Energy, 2 Water, Manufactured Guns | Deploys defensive guards and strike squads. |

A crucial rule in this **Build and Conquer starters guide** is to never deploy costly military assets before your civilian economy is fully established. Your initial barracks is free to place, but recruiting soldiers and maintaining automated defenses drains money every 30 seconds. 

As other players expand across the server, secure your airspace and borders. Early investments in perimeter defenses—such as Patriot missile batteries, Iron Dome interceptors, and underground deployment tunnels—will safeguard your city's factories from surprise raids.

## Troubleshooting Common Early-Game Mistakes

| Common Issue | Probable Root Cause | Player-Reported Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Not enough available workers" error** | All existing residents are tied up in shops or agriculture. | Drop an apartment block or pause non-essential commercial jobs. |
| **Building outlines remain red** | The structure is further than 4 studs from a road, or lacks power/water. | Snap a dirt road connector closer and verify power lines connect directly. |
| **Negative cash flow every 30 seconds** | Upkeep on military or empty services exceeds tax revenue. | Upgrade existing shops to Level 2 and demolish unstaffed infrastructure. |
| **Barracks refuses to spawn troops** | Weapons have been researched, but not manufactured in a factory. | Build a Military Factory, supply iron, and produce physical inventory. |
| **No citizens moving into homes** | Residential buildings lack clean water connections. | Check that your Water Tower pipeline connects directly to housing lots. |
