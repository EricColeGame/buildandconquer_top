## Getting Started: Foundations, Roads, and Essential Utilities

When you claim an open plot marked by cyan boundaries, you begin with $2,500 in starter funds. This initial cash reserve looks generous, but it depletes rapidly if you lay expensive roads or place structures before securing utilities. Every functioning building requires a direct road connection, electricity, and water before citizens move in or work.

When applying early-game Build and Conquer tips and tricks, always use free resources first. The game grants every player one free Wind Turbine and one free Water Tower under the Services tab. Place these right next to your road so their connection aura reaches adjacent zones immediately.

```
[Plot Backbone: Road]
  ├── [Free Wind Turbine] (Power)
  ├── [Free Water Tower]  (Water)
  ├── [Residential Zone]  (Within 4 studs of road)
  └── [Commercial Shop]   (Within 4 studs of road)
```

Remember the **4-stud rule**: any building placed farther than 4 studs from an active road cannot activate. When drawing roads, you can save money by laying dirt roads ($0) to outline your grid, reserving paid two-lane roads ($10 per unit) for main transit paths.

## Economic Mastery: Balancing Housing, Commerce, and Upkeep

One of the most vital Build and Conquer tips and tricks involves managing your revenue cycle. Net cash pays out every **30 seconds**. Your total profit equals shop earnings, residential taxes, and citizen productivity minus building maintenance and service upkeep.

Over-expanding housing without matching jobs drains your treasury. Citizens must be matched to commercial and industrial buildings that suit their economic tier. A basic Shop requires two available workers to operate. Once staffed, it generates recurring cash every 30 seconds.

## Urban Layout and Zoning: Optimizing Efficiency

Urban planning is another core area where advanced Build and Conquer tips and tricks make an enormous difference. Commercial buildings produce a happiness dynamic that can either empower or hurt your neighborhoods. 

According to community observations, standard Shops grant **+2 happiness** to residences within 40 studs, but impose a **-2 happiness penalty** if placed within 20 studs due to commercial noise. Maintain a green buffer zone or road lane between bedrooms and storefronts to capture the bonus while avoiding the penalty.

```
[Residential Sector] <--- 20 to 40 studs buffer ---> [Commercial Sector]
(Avoid placing shops directly adjacent to residential walls to protect happiness)
```

Additionally, utility lines must be routed thoughtfully. Wind turbines and solar panels generate electricity, but electrical flow cannot pass through water buildings, and water pipelines cannot cross electrical substations directly without proper clearance. Keep utility corridors separated along designated utility alleys.

## Research Progression and Military Logistics

Once your economy registers consistent positive cash flow every 30 seconds, shift focus toward technological superiority. The Small Research Office costs $400, but it requires **15 available workers**, 10 energy, and 8 water before placement is permitted. 

Applying tactical Build and Conquer tips and tricks to your research pipeline ensures you do not trigger worker shortages. A single Middle Class Apartment provides +18 residents in one build, immediately fulfilling the labor quota required by the Small Research Office.

```
[1x Middle Class Apartment] (+18 Workers)
         │
         ▼
[Small Research Office] (Requires 15 Workers)
         │
         ▼
[SLOPARMS Tech Tree: M16 Project Started]
```

Inside the SLOPARMS research screen, you can fund military projects. Only one technology researches at a time. The entry-level **M16** project costs up to $1,000 in funding and completes in roughly 5 minutes. Completing this project unlocks advanced arms like the Bloated M16, TRG-21 Interceptor, and Minimi, which you can assemble inside a Military Factory.

Based on community wiki data, the military tech tree expands from light infantry into ballistic missiles and interceptors:

Never deploy advanced defensive batteries like the Iron Dome or Patriot PAC-3 until your raw resource extraction (Iron, Coal, and Oil) and refining networks run smoothly. Defensive structures protect you from aggressive neighbors, but their steep power draw can crash an unprepared power grid.

## Leveling Up Your City: Fast-Tracking Progression

In Build and Conquer, your City Level dictates which blueprints and industrial buildings unlock. According to community reports, leveling criteria are strictly tied to **population milestones** and sustained infrastructure support.

These city-leveling Build and Conquer tips and tricks allow you to bypass bottlenecks and upgrade your settlement rank rapidly:

1. **Prioritize Multi-Family Housing**: Constructing individual poor houses takes too much land and road footage. Transition to Low Income and Middle Class Apartments as soon as they unlock to hit population thresholds.
2. **Pre-Build Utility Surpluses**: Always install an extra Wind Turbine or Water Tank *before* your residential meters drop below 10% surplus capacity. If water drops to zero, residents stop arriving.
3. **Automate Raw Resource Chains**: Place Mines and Oil Pumps early. Raw coal fuels advanced Coal Power Plants, while crude oil feeds Refineries to create fuel for vehicle logistics and military factories.
4. **Maintain Reserve Capital**: Keep at least $500 to $1,000 in liquid reserves. If a sudden upkeep spike puts your balance into negative numbers, construction freezes until the next 30-second payout recovers your finances.

### What are the most important Build and Conquer tips and tricks for earning money early?
The single most important tip is to pace your growth. Place a two-lane road, drop your free Wind Turbine and Water Tower, build one starter house, and place a Shop within 4 studs of the road. Upgrade the Shop to Level 2 for $200 instead of purchasing multiple buildings, and never place a barracks or research lab until your net profit is positive across consecutive 30-second payout cycles.

### Why does my Small Research Office display an error when I try to place it?
The Small Research Office requires 15 available, unemployed workers. If your population is entirely employed in shops or farms, the game will block placement. Build a Middle Class Apartment (+18 residents) connected to road, power, and water to supply the necessary workforce before placing the office.

### How do I defend my city against incoming player attacks?
Early on, keep a low military profile to save capital. As your city level rises, invest in SLOPARMS missile research to unlock anti-air infrastructure, such as the Patriot Battery, Iron Dome, or Patriot PAC-3. Ensure your power grid has significant surplus wattage so your defensive batteries remain active around the clock.
