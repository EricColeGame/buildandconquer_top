## Road Grids and Utility Networks: The 4-Stud Rule

The single most common error new mayors commit is placing buildings too far from infrastructure. In this **Build and Conquer beginner guide**, our primary rule of thumb is the **4-stud rule**: every functional house, shop, and utility plant must sit within 4 studs of an active road.

```
[ Two-Lane Road ] <--- within 4 studs ---> [ House / Shop / Facility ]
```

When placing a structure, observe the placement ghost:
- **Green Outline:** The building has proper road frontage, valid terrain clearance, and utility connectivity.
- **Red Outline:** The building lacks road access, exceeds network range, or conflicts with another footprint.

### Road Strategy: Dirt Roads vs. Paved Roads

Standard two-lane roads cost $10 per unit, while basic dirt roads are completely free ($0). When planning large city blocks, draw the outer boundaries and layout grids with dirt road. Once your economy stabilizes, you can replace heavy traffic corridors with paved asphalt.

| Road Type | Construction Cost | Primary Role | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dirt Road** | $0 | Low-cost layout zoning | Grid sketching, farm access, perimeter roads |
| **Two-Lane Road** | $10 per unit | Standard urban transit | Main avenues, commercial strips, factory connections |

### Separating Power and Water Grids

Power and water do not share transmission logic. Energy from wind turbines, solar panels, and coal plants flows along direct connections, but electricity cannot bridge across water installations. Similarly, water pipes and tanks need continuous pathways. Keep utility clusters organized so power generation lines do not bottleneck behind your water towers.

## Establishing Early Cash Flow: Housing, Shops, and Upkeep

Money in *Build and Conquer* does not trickle in continuously; it pays out in lump sums every **30 seconds**. When the 30-second cycle hits, the server tallies your gross revenues (commercial sales, worker productivity, residential taxes) and deducts operating expenses (facility upkeep, utility grid costs, military maintenance).

If your expenses outstrip your output, your cash balance drains into negative territory. Building a solid financial base early is the foundation of any helpful **Build and Conquer beginner guide**.

### Residential and Commercial Balance

Commercial buildings generate the bulk of your taxable cash flow, but they cannot open their doors without staff. To staff a store, you must provide residential housing within your utility zone. 

Furthermore, citizens adhere to wealth classes. Low-income citizens work in lower-tier jobs and shop at low-tier businesses. Middle-class citizens require higher-tier apartments and frequent mid-level stores.

```
[ Residential Zone ] ---> Produces Workers ---> [ Commercial Shops ] ---> Generates Revenue (Every 30s)
```

| Starter Building | Base Price | Community-Reported Output | Resource Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Residential House 1** | $100 | +3 Citizens, +$20 per cycle | 1 Energy, 1 Water |
| **Middle Class Apartment** | $450 | +18 Citizens, +$130 per cycle | 5 Energy, 4 Water |
| **Commercial Shop** | Starter tier | Generates early revenue | 2 Workers, road connection |
| **Farm Field** | $100 | +1 Food per cycle | 1 Worker, must touch Farm/Wheat |

### Managing Commercial Placement Debuffs

When zoning your first commercial strip, pay attention to building auras. According to player experience reports, placing a retail shop right next to a family home can lower property happiness.
- **Within 20 studs:** -2 happiness penalty to nearby houses.
- **Between 21 and 40 studs:** +2 commercial access bonus to houses.

Place your residential houses along a parallel side street roughly 25 to 35 studs away from your primary commercial road. This keeps housing within the positive aura ring while avoiding noise penalties. Once your first basic Shop is constructed, select it and purchase the Level 2 upgrade for $200 to significantly boost its earnings without adding extra footprint.

## Hitting 15 Workers and Unlocking Military Research

Your civilian city ultimately serves one purpose: funding and engineering your defense forces. Modern military armaments cannot be bought off the shelf; every single weapon platform, missile battery, and armored vehicle must be researched and manufactured locally.

To begin this process, you must construct the **Small Research Office** (labeled SLOPARMS in the UI). However, placing this building requires **15 available workers**. If all your citizens are currently employed at grocery shops or farms, the building tool displays an error: *"Not enough available workers."*

### Fast-Tracking Population Growth

Single-family homes provide only 3 citizens each. Constructing five separate starter homes requires multiple road extensions and eats up valuable plot space. Instead, save funds for a denser housing unit:

1. Lay a short road spur branching off your main street.
2. Confirm your initial wind turbine and water tank have surplus capacity (at least 6 energy and 5 water).
3. Place a **Middle Class Apartment** ($450 community wiki data), which grants +18 population in a single lot.
4. Verify the workers register as unemployed in your city management overview.
5. Place the Small Research Office beside your paved road.

| Early Milestone | Requirements | Immediate Unlocks | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **City Level 1** | Plot claim, 1 Road, Starter Utilities | Basic Houses, Dirt Roads, Shop | Secure positive cash flow |
| **Workforce Tier 1** | 15 Unemployed Workers | Small Research Office | Upgrade Shop to Lvl 2 |
| **SLOPARMS Branch 1** | Research Office, $1,000 research fund | M16 Infantry Rifle | Assign funding immediately |
| **Food Supply** | Farm Field + Wheat Farm | Baseline food production | Prevent civil starvation |

### Initiating Your First Research Project

Once your Small Research Office is active, click the structure to view the SLOPARMS technology tree. Research runs one project at a time and requires continuous monetary allocation.

Select the **M16** under Infantry (Rank I). Based on player reports, the research bar takes approximately 5 minutes to complete with up to $1,000 invested. Completing this unlock paves the way for advanced weaponry, including the Minimi, TRG-21 Interceptor, and Bloated M16.

## Expanding Into Industry, Raw Materials, and Territorial Defense

Once your city produces steady net income every 30 seconds, shift focus toward primary extraction and industrial refining. Advanced units require physical materials rather than raw cash.

### The Industrial Production Chain

The industrial loop in *Build and Conquer* transforms raw underground assets into refined fuels and finished consumer goods.

```
[ Oil Pump / Mine ] ---> Raw Oil & Ore ---> [ Refinery / Factory ] ---> Fuel & Military Parts
```

- **Mines:** Placed on natural deposits to extract Coal and Iron.
- **Oil Pumps:** Extract crude petroleum for processing.
- **Refineries (Small & Medium):** Convert crude oil into usable vehicle Fuel.
- **Factories:** Combine Iron and intermediate parts into consumer goods and military armaments.

### Defensive Infrastructure

As your city becomes wealthier, other players will eye your installations. Leaving your borders open invites devastating drone and missile raids.

| Military Asset | Category | Community Notes | Tactical Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barracks** | Personnel | Trains rifle recruits | Base defense and patrol |
| **Shahed Drone** | Drone Warfare | Low-cost guided payload | Long-range harassment |
| **SRBM / MRBM Launchers** | Ballistic Missiles | High damage against enemy structures | Retaliatory strikes |
| **Patriot PAC-3 / Iron Dome**| Air Defense | Intercepts incoming missiles and aircraft | Core base protection |
| **Military Car Storage** | Armored Vehicles | Houses ground combat vehicles | Counter-offensive assaults |

Prioritize installing an early air defense umbrella—such as an Iron Dome or Patriot installation—near your central power plants and research offices. Losing your main power grid shuts down all water pumps, halting city income instantly.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Beta V0.1

Even experienced city-builder fans stumble over specific mechanics in *Build and Conquer*. Applying the hard lessons documented in this **Build and Conquer beginner guide** will save your budget from early collapse.

- **Over-Zoning Housing Without Jobs:** Building endless residential rows without shops or factories creates idle citizens that consume food and water while contributing minimal tax revenue.
- **Expanding Military Before Economy:** Barracks and missile launch pads have heavy continuous maintenance costs. Building an army before generating +$300 net profit per cycle will bankrupt you.
- **Ignoring Utility Bottlenecks:** Always verify you have 10–15 surplus units of energy and water before dropping new apartment blocks or research offices.
- **Neglecting the 4-Stud Limit:** If a newly placed building displays an orange or red alert, check whether its entrance is tilted away from the road. The entrance must face the road within 4 studs.
- **Searching for Secret Promo Codes:** As confirmed by the developers at The Slop Company, there are no official promo codes active for *Build and Conquer* during V0.1. Avoid suspicious websites promising free cash or robux codes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my building show a red outline when placing it?
A red outline indicates an invalid placement. The most common cause is exceeding the 4-stud distance limit from an active road. Other common causes include insufficient power, lack of water pipeline reach, terrain obstruction, or not having enough free workers available in your city overview.

### How often do I earn income in Build and Conquer?
Net income pays out once every 30 seconds. Your total earnings equal your combined residential taxes and commercial shop revenues minus municipal upkeep, utility power plant costs, and active military maintenance.

### What is the fastest way to get 15 workers for the Small Research Office?
Instead of building five individual starter houses, save your funds and construct a single **Middle Class Apartment** (community reports list cost at roughly $450). This adds +18 citizens in one footprint, easily satisfying the 15-worker threshold required to place the SLOPARMS research office.
